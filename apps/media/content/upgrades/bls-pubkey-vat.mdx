# BLS Pubkey and the Validator Admission Ticket

**Updated October 2026** • Solana Foundation

Solana mainnet validators register a BLS pubkey on-chain, the cryptographic
prerequisite for [Alpenglow](/upgrades/alpenglow) consensus.
The feature gate that enables this
([SIMD-0387](https://github.com/solana-foundation/solana-improvement-documents/blob/main/proposals/0387-bls-pubkey-management-in-vote-account.md))
activated on mainnet on July 8, 2026. The Validator Admission Ticket
([SIMD-0357](https://github.com/solana-foundation/solana-improvement-documents/blob/main/proposals/0357-alpenglow_validator_admission_ticket.md),
VAT), which enforces the requirement, activated on mainnet on July 22, 2026: any
validator without a registered BLS pubkey now stops participating in
consensus.

The VAT feature activation did not turn on Alpenglow itself. Until Alpenglow,
VAT only gated admission into the validator set based on the existence of the
BLS pubkey. Alpenglow's consensus switch landed separately in Agave 4.3, at
epoch 1064 on October 21, 2026, and with it the complete VAT feature took
effect. Validators now have 0.8 SOL deducted from their _vote account_ at the
start of each epoch. Votes are no longer onchain transactions, so there are no
longer fees for voting.

**Keep at least 0.8 SOL in your vote account, above its rent-exempt minimum.**
If the fee is not paid, your validator does not vote or produce blocks in the
next epoch. See [what validators need now](#what-validators-need-now-that-alpenglow-is-live).

| Devnet / Testnet Activation | Live — both BLS pubkeys and VAT are active on devnet and testnet                                                     |

## Technical Details

### What a BLS Pubkey Is, and Why Alpenglow Needs One

Under TowerBFT, Solana validators voted using ed25519 signatures, verified one
at a time. Alpenglow's voting algorithm, Votor, needs to combine thousands of
individual validator votes into a single succinct certificate rather than
verifying each vote independently. BLS (Boneh–Lynn–Shacham) signatures
support this kind of aggregation: many BLS signatures over the same message
can be combined into one aggregate signature that still proves every
contributing validator voted. Ed25519 does not support this, which is why
Alpenglow requires a separate BLS keypair for each validator rather than
reusing the existing vote-account identity.

### Registering Your BLS Pubkey On-Chain

Registering a BLS pubkey requires the **Solana CLI version
4.1.0 or higher**. The registration flow, documented in
[Anza's vote account guide](https://docs.anza.xyz/operations/guides/vote-accounts#set-the-bls-public-key),
runs through the CLI:

```bash
# Derive the BLS pubkey from your authorized voter keypair
solana-keygen bls_pubkey <AUTHORIZED_VOTER_KEYPAIR>

# Register it on-chain against your vote account
solana vote-authorize-voter-checked <VOTE_ACCOUNT> <AUTHORIZED_VOTER_KEYPAIR> <AUTHORIZED_VOTER_KEYPAIR>

# Confirm it took effect
solana vote-account <VOTE_ACCOUNT> | grep "BLS Public Key"
```

Under the hood, this submits a new `VoterWithBLS` vote-authorization variant
that carries both the 48-byte compressed BLS pubkey and a 96-byte proof of
possession. The proof of possession signs a domain-separated message binding
the vote account to the BLS key, which stops an attacker from registering a
rogue key that could interfere with aggregate signature verification. This
means updating a BLS pubkey does not require changing your authorized
voter — it is a distinct, additive registration.

### The Validator Admission Ticket (VAT)

VAT is a separate feature gate that changes how validators are admitted
into the active set each epoch. The VAT feature has two states: before
Alpenglow was enabled and after.

_VAT Before Alpenglow_

From July 22, 2026 until Alpenglow activated, the VAT feature gate required
that all voting validators have a BLS pubkey registered and enforced a limit of
2,000 validators. TowerBFT remained in effect, so votes were still transactions
that landed on-chain, and voting validators were still charged about 1 SOL per
epoch in aggregate voting costs.

_VAT After Alpenglow_

Since Alpenglow activated in epoch 1064, every voting validator included for
the next epoch is charged a flat 0.8 SOL fee per epoch, burned directly to the
incinerator rather than redistributed to block proposers as transaction fees
are. That figure is intentionally close to the old voting costs (roughly 1 SOL
per epoch).

The fee is taken from the vote account, not the identity account that paid
vote transaction fees under TowerBFT, and the 0.8 SOL is in addition to the
vote account's rent-exempt minimum. The fee is paid one epoch ahead: the first
charge, at the start of epoch 1064, paid for participation in epoch 1065. If
the vote account cannot pay it, the validator is not admitted for the next
epoch and does not vote or produce blocks. The fee is charged every epoch, so
the balance has to stay funded, not be funded once. Routing block revenue to
the vote account can do that automatically; see
[Fund the VAT with commission revenue](https://docs.anza.xyz/operations/guides/vote-accounts#fund-the-vat-with-commission-revenue).

### What Happens Now That VAT Is Active

With the VAT feature gate and Alpenglow live on mainnet:

- Validators without a registered BLS pubkey are excluded from the
  admitted set and stop voting — they do not participate in consensus and
  stop earning inflation rewards.
- Validators with a BLS pubkey registered but outside the top 2,000 by
  stake are excluded from the admitted set for that epoch.
- Each admitted validator pays the 0.8 SOL VAT fee from its vote account at
  the start of every epoch for the next one.
- VAT itself did not turn on Alpenglow consensus. [SIMD-0326 - Alpenglow: new consensus algorithm](https://github.com/solana-foundation/solana-improvement-documents/blob/main/proposals/0326-alpenglow.md)
  activated on mainnet separately, in epoch 1064, which started on October 21, 2026, at approximately 13:36 UTC. See [When Alpenglow Activated on Mainnet](/upgrades/alpenglow#when-alpenglow-activated-on-mainnet).

If your voting validator does not yet have a BLS pubkey registered, it is
already losing consensus participation and inflation rewards — register
immediately using the steps above.

### What Validators Need Now That Alpenglow Is Live

Registering a BLS pubkey is not the only requirement. Since Alpenglow activated
in epoch 1064 (October 21, 2026, approximately 13:36 UTC), validator operators
must also:

1. **Run an Agave-based client:** v4.3.2 of Agave, Jito-Solana, or Harmonic
   Salsa. Firedancer and Frankendancer were not supported for the migration.
2. **Hold at least 0.8 SOL in the vote account, above its rent-exempt
   minimum,** to pay the VAT fee. Check it
   with `solana balance <VOTE_ACCOUNT_ADDRESS>`, top it up with
   `solana transfer <VOTE_ACCOUNT_ADDRESS> <AMOUNT>`, and leave enough behind
   for the next fee whenever you withdraw from it. The fee is charged at the
   start of every epoch and pays for the next one. To keep the account funded
   automatically, route block revenue to it with
   `solana vote-update-commission-collector`; the change takes two epochs to
   apply.

Both are covered in the
[Alpenglow validator operator details](/upgrades/alpenglow#breaking-change-your-vote-account-pays-the-vat-fee).

## About This Upgrade

BLS pubkeys and VAT were the prerequisites for Alpenglow, not the full
Alpenglow feature set. The new consensus algorithm, with 150ms finality and
off-chain voting, arrived in Agave 4.3 and activated on mainnet in epoch 1064.
The migration only worked because the validator set was ready before the
switch: every validator needed a BLS key registered, and the network needed a
defined, capped admission process in place first. With Alpenglow live,
operators also need an Agave-based client and at least 0.8 SOL above the
rent-exempt minimum in the vote account for the VAT fee each epoch. Validator
operators who have not registered a BLS pubkey are currently unable to
participate in consensus and are not receiving on-chain inflationary rewards
or block fees.
