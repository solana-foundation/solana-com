# BLS Pubkey and the Validator Admission Ticket

**Updated October 2026** • Solana Foundation

Solana mainnet validators can now create a BLS pubkey on-chain, the
cryptographic prerequisite for [Alpenglow](/upgrades/alpenglow) consensus.
The feature gate that enables this
([SIMD-0387](https://github.com/solana-foundation/solana-improvement-documents/blob/main/proposals/0387-bls-pubkey-management-in-vote-account.md))
activated on mainnet on July 8, 2026. The Validator Admission Ticket
([SIMD-0357](https://github.com/solana-foundation/solana-improvement-documents/blob/main/proposals/0357-alpenglow_validator_admission_ticket.md),
VAT), which enforces the requirement, activated on mainnet on July 22, 2026: any
validator without a registered BLS pubkey now stops participating in
consensus.

The VAT feature activation does not turn on Alpenglow itself. Alpenglow's
code ships in Agave 4.3, and its consensus switch activates on mainnet
separately with Agave 4.4. The VAT feature only gates
admission into the validator set based on the existence of the BLS pubkey. Once
the full Alpenglow consensus switch happens with Agave 4.4, the complete VAT
feature will be enabled. When both VAT and Alpenglow are enabled, validators
will have 0.8 SOL deducted from their _vote account_ at the start of each epoch.
Votes will no longer be onchain transactions, so there will no longer be fees
for voting.

**Before Alpenglow activates, hold at least 0.8 SOL in your vote account, above
its rent-exempt minimum.** If
the fee is not paid, your validator does not vote or produce blocks in the next epoch.
See [what to do before Alpenglow activates](#what-to-do-before-alpenglow-activates).

## Technical Details

### What a BLS Pubkey Is, and Why Alpenglow Needs One

Solana validators currently vote using ed25519 signatures, verified one at a
time. Alpenglow's voting algorithm, Votor, needs to combine thousands of
individual validator votes into a single succinct certificate rather than
verifying each vote independently. BLS (Boneh–Lynn–Shacham) signatures
support this kind of aggregation: many BLS signatures over the same message
can be combined into one aggregate signature that still proves every
contributing validator voted. Ed25519 does not support this, which is why
Alpenglow requires a separate BLS keypair for each validator rather than
reusing the existing vote-account identity.

### Registering Your BLS Pubkey On-Chain

Registering a BLS pubkey requires the **Solana CLI version
4.1.0 or higher**. The registration flow, documented in
[Anza's vote account guide](https://docs.anza.xyz/operations/guides/vote-accounts#set-the-bls-public-key),
runs through the CLI:

```bash
# Derive the BLS pubkey from your authorized voter keypair
solana-keygen bls_pubkey <AUTHORIZED_VOTER_KEYPAIR>

# Register it on-chain against your vote account
solana vote-authorize-voter-checked <VOTE_ACCOUNT> <AUTHORIZED_VOTER_KEYPAIR> <AUTHORIZED_VOTER_KEYPAIR>

# Confirm it took effect
solana vote-account <VOTE_ACCOUNT> | grep "BLS Public Key"
```

Under the hood, this submits a new `VoterWithBLS` vote-authorization variant
that carries both the 48-byte compressed BLS pubkey and a 96-byte proof of
possession. The proof of possession signs a domain-separated message binding
the vote account to the BLS key, which stops an attacker from registering a
rogue key that could interfere with aggregate signature verification. This
means updating a BLS pubkey does not require changing your authorized
voter — it is a distinct, additive registration.

### The Validator Admission Ticket (VAT)

VAT is a separate feature gate that changes how validators are admitted
into the active set each epoch. The VAT feature activation has two different
states: before Alpenglow is enabled and after Alpenglow is enabled.

_VAT Before Alpenglow_

In the first state, before Alpenglow is enabled, the VAT feature gate will
require that all voting validators have a BLS pubkey enabled and it will also
enforce a limit of 2,000 validators. Since TowerBFT, the current Solana consensus
algorithm, will remain in effect, votes will continue to be transactions that
land on-chain. Voting validators will continue to be charged about 1 SOL per
epoch in aggregate voting costs.

_VAT After Alpenglow_

In the second state, after Alpenglow is
enabled, every voting validator included for the next epoch is charged a
flat 0.8 SOL fee per epoch, burned directly to the incinerator rather than
redistributed to block proposers as transaction and vote fees are today. That
figure is intentionally close to existing voting costs (roughly 1 SOL per epoch).

The fee is taken from the vote account, not the identity account that pays
vote transaction fees today, and the 0.8 SOL is in addition to the vote
account's rent-exempt minimum. The fee is paid one epoch ahead: the first
charge, at the start of epoch 1064, pays for participation in epoch 1065. If
the vote account cannot pay it, the validator is not admitted for the next
epoch and does not vote or produce blocks. The fee is charged every epoch, so
the balance has to stay funded, not be funded once. Routing block revenue to
the vote account can do that automatically; see
[Fund the VAT with commission revenue](https://docs.anza.xyz/operations/guides/vote-accounts#fund-the-vat-with-commission-revenue).

### What Happens Now That VAT Is Active

With the VAT feature gate live on mainnet:

- Validators without a registered BLS pubkey are excluded from the
  admitted set and stop voting — they do not participate in consensus and
  stop earning inflation rewards.
- Validators with a BLS pubkey registered but outside the top 2,000 by
  stake are excluded from the admitted set for that epoch.
- None of this turns on Alpenglow consensus itself. [SIMD-0326 - Alpenglow: new consensus algorithm](https://github.com/solana-foundation/solana-improvement-documents/blob/main/proposals/0326-alpenglow.md)
  activates on mainnet in epoch 1064, which starts on October 21, 2026, at approximately 15:00 UTC. See [When Alpenglow Activates on Mainnet](/upgrades/alpenglow#when-alpenglow-activates-on-mainnet).

If your voting validator does not yet have a BLS pubkey registered, it is
already losing consensus participation and inflation rewards — register
immediately using the steps above.

### What To Do Before Alpenglow Activates

Registering a BLS pubkey is not the last step. Before Alpenglow activates in
epoch 1064 (October 21, 2026, approximately 15:00 UTC),
validator operators must also:

1. **Run an Agave-based client:** Agave, Jito-Solana, or Harmonic Salsa, on a
   release built on Agave 4.3.0. Firedancer and Frankendancer are not supported
   during the migration.
2. **Hold at least 0.8 SOL in the vote account, above its rent-exempt
   minimum,** to pay the VAT fee. Check it
   with `solana balance <VOTE_ACCOUNT_ADDRESS>`, top it up with
   `solana transfer <VOTE_ACCOUNT_ADDRESS> <AMOUNT>`, and leave enough behind
   for the next fee whenever you withdraw from it. The first fee is charged at
   the start of epoch 1064 and pays for epoch 1065. To keep the account funded
   automatically, route block revenue to it with
   `solana vote-update-commission-collector`; the change takes two epochs to
   apply.

Both are covered in the
[Alpenglow validator operator details](/upgrades/alpenglow#breaking-change-your-vote-account-pays-the-vat-fee).

## About This Upgrade

BLS pubkeys and VAT are important prerequisites, not the full Alpenglow feature
set. The new Alpenglow consensus algorithm with 150ms finality and off-chain
voting ships in Agave 4.3 and activates on mainnet with Agave 4.4. The migration to Alpenglow only works if the
validator set is ready before the switch: every validator needs a BLS key
registered, and the network needs a defined, capped admission process to move
into place first. Both prerequisites are now live on mainnet. Before Alpenglow
itself activates, operators also need an Agave-based client and at least 0.8 SOL
above the rent-exempt minimum in the vote account for the VAT fee. Validator
operators who have not registered a BLS pubkey are currently unable to
participate in consensus and are not receiving on-chain inflationary rewards
or block fees.
