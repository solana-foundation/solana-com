Solana increased maximum block capacity by 66% in July as its onchain equity market added new issuer-backed shares and around-the-clock trading. SK Hynix reached Solana on the day of its $26.5 billion Nasdaq offering, while Robinhood, Strategy, Intel shares became available through Sunrise and Backpack Securities.

Payments reached more businesses and consumers. E\*TRADE opened spot SOL trading to eligible clients, Ramp introduced stablecoin accounts and payments with support for Solana, and KSNET agreed to integrate Solana Pay across a Korean merchant network with more than 330,000 locations. The Linux Foundation opened the x402 Foundation, with the Solana Foundation joining Visa and Stripe as a premier member. At the World Series of Poker, Solana processed $22.9 million in buy-ins during its first season.

## **July Snapshot**

- **100M compute units per block:** Solana raised the block limit from 60M to 100M CUs, a 66% increase in maximum block capacity
- **$3.73B in RWA value:** Solana's real-world asset value reached a new all-time high near the end of July
- **313K+ RWA holders:** The number of unique addresses holding tokenized real-world assets on Solana exceeded 313,000 by month-end
- **$22.9M in WSOP buy-ins:** 6,176 tournament entries were paid through Solana during the 2026 World Series of Poker
- **$1.12B in U.S.-listed Solana fund flows:** U.S.-listed Solana funds had $1.12 billion in cumulative net flows by July 31, including seed assets and a prior-product conversion

## **Tokenized Assets and Capital Markets**

### **Public equities added around-the-clock markets**

SK Hynix began trading on Nasdaq on July 10 after a[ ](https://apnews.com/article/73f13a85ae00e30bad0540281bbe44f3)$26.5 billion offering, [the largest initial U.S. share sale by a foreign company](https://apnews.com/article/73f13a85ae00e30bad0540281bbe44f3). The company became [available on Solana](https://solana.com/news/the-sun-rises-in-seoul-and-trades-on-solana-skhy-is-now-live) the same day through Sunrise with Backpack Securities, alongside separate products from xStocks and Ondo Finance. The Backpack Securities version is redeemable one for one for the underlying security entitlement.

Sunrise brought additional Backpack Securities-issued equities to Solana during July, including [Robinhood (HOOD](https://x.com/solana/status/2077754737211130078)), Intel[ (INTC](https://x.com/sunrise/status/2079561377791017418)), Strategy ([MSTR](https://x.com/solana/status/2082100191768031600)) and RoboStrategy ([BOT](https://x.com/solana/status/2072682298525585885)). Backpack also[ opened 24/7 trading for selected U.S. equities](https://learn.backpack.exchange/blog/24-7-real-us-equities-and-global-market-access-armani-ferrante-on-cnbc).

Ondo Finance activated[ 24/7 minting and redemption for six tokenized stocks and ETFs on Solana](https://www.cryptowisser.com/news/real-247-trading-is-live-on-solana/) on July 9. The initial set included tokenized versions of SPY, QQQ, Circle, Nvidia, Tesla, and Alphabet. On July 17,[ Ondo expanded the 24/7 set to 16 assets](https://x.com/ondofinance/status/2078110296971915767), adding AMD, Intel, Micron, TSMC, Marvell, SpaceX, SanDisk, Broadcom, SK Hynix, and Arm Holdings. Its Intel product uses Ondo's issuance structure, separate from Backpack's INTC.

### **Securitize brought issuer-sponsored SECZ to Solana**

Securitize began trading on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker SECZ on July 2 and[ made tokenized SECZ available on Solana](https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2026/Tokenizing-SECZ-Securitize-Brings-Its-Own-Public-Stock-Onchain-at-Listing-Day/default.aspx). The tokenized position represents the same common stock that trades on the NYSE rather than a separate share class. Tokenized SECZ is available to eligible investors through Securitize's regulated platform.

The launch made Securitize the first newly public company to bring its own stock onchain from the beginning of its public life.

### **Funds and credit products broadened the RWA mix**

On July 13, SBI Holdings [announced](https://www.sbigroup.co.jp/news/2026/0713_16478.html) plans to create a Japan-originated onchain financial market and support JPYSC distribution, tokenized corporate bonds and funds, cross-border settlement, and institutional onchain services on Solana.

On July 14, SBI Global Asset Management and DigiFT launched[ JX on Solana](https://x.com/solana/status/2077251419103740171), bringing a listed-equity strategy from a Japanese asset manager onchain for the first time. JX gives eligible accredited and institutional investors access to SBI’s Japanese high-dividend equity strategy.[ Subscriptions accept USDC](https://www.sbigroup.co.jp/news/2026/0715_16484.html), with JPYSC support planned after the yen stablecoin becomes available on public blockchain rails.

KAIO brought a tokenized share class of the Mubadala Capital Alternative Solutions Fund to Solana, Base, and Sui. The strategy had[ approximately $75 million in onchain commitments](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/07/23/abu-dhabi-s-mubadala-capital-joins-tokenization-push-as-coinbase-takes-stake-in-onchain-fund) at launch.

Coinbase Asset Management's USD Stablecoin Yield Fund also went live on Solana through Superstate. The[ CUSHY share class held $25 million in net asset value on Solana](https://superstate.com/assets/cushy) at launch. The private credit strategy is open to eligible qualified purchasers.

Hastra launched AUTO, a token backed by near-prime U.S. auto loans originated by Agora and delivered onchain through Figure Forge.[ AUTO entered an isolated Kamino market](https://x.com/HastraFi/status/2082524249299063033).

By the end of July,[ ](https://app.rwa.xyz/networks/solana)Solana reached a record [$3.73 billion in real-world asset value](https://app.rwa.xyz/networks/solana) and more than 313,000 RWA holders.

## **Regulated Market Access**

Morgan Stanley Investment Management[ launched the Morgan Stanley Solana Trust, ticker MSOL](https://www.morganstanley.com/im/en-us/financial-advisor/insights/press-release/msim-expands-etp-offerings-with-launch-of-ethereum-and-solana-exchange-traded-products.html), on NYSE Arca on July 28. The trust holds SOL and intends to stake a portion of its holdings. It is available through brokerage accounts.

![](/uploads/posts/solana-ecosystem-roundup-july-2026/Group%20243.webp)

By July 31, U.S.-listed Solana funds had [$1.12 billion in cumulative net flows](https://farside.co.uk/sol/), including seed assets and Grayscale's conversion of a prior product.

E\*TRADE from Morgan Stanley[ opened spot trading for SOL, bitcoin, and ether](https://www.morganstanley.com/press-releases/etrade-completes-crypto-spot-trading-rollout) on July 16. Eligible clients can buy, sell, and hold the assets through a linked zerohash account and view them alongside traditional investments in the E\*TRADE interface.

## **DeFi and Onchain Markets**

Jito[ opened JTX to the public on July 21](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/jito-labs-launches-jtx-a-platform-purpose-built-for-professional-traders-302830219.html). The self-custodial trading application launched with spot markets spanning native tokens, major crypto assets, and tokenized equities.

[Jupiter introduced Earn on Recurring](https://x.com/JupiterExchange/status/2076025979492028858), which routes idle USDC in active recurring orders into Jupiter Lend until each scheduled purchase executes.

Several lending and yield markets reached new scale during the month:

- Exponent [crossed $100 million in total value locked](https://x.com/ExponentFinance/status/2080300887550124047) 44 days after its V2 launch
- The OnRe market on Kamino [crossed $200 million in total market size](https://x.com/kamino/status/2080350843727942118)
- Sunrise also brought[ Solstice’s SLX](https://x.com/solana/status/2074500564625522867),[ MORPHO](https://x.com/solana/status/2075221595313115412) and[ ](https://x.com/solana/status/2075728392628916624)[CASHCAT](http://cashcat.to) to Solana during July

## **Stablecoins and Payments**

Ramp[ introduced stablecoin accounts](https://ramp.com/blog/introducing-stablecoins) and international payments with Solana as a [supported network](https://support.ramp.com/ramp-stablecoin-account-overview) for stablecoin deposits. Eligible businesses can hold stablecoins, pay vendors in USDC or USDT from a dollar bank account.

KSNET signed a memorandum of understanding with the Solana Foundation to[ integrate Solana Pay](https://x.com/solana/status/2082827736360583317) across a network of more than 330,000 merchants. The Korean payment provider processes more than $4 billion in monthly volume. The agreement also covers testing x402 payments for agent-driven commerce.

At the World Series of Poker, Solana payments reached 6,176 entries and[ $22,889,450 in total buy-ins](https://solanacompass.com/news/solana-processed-229m-in-wsop-buy-ins-during-its-first-year-at-the-tables).

## **Agentic Commerce**

The Linux Foundation[ opened the x402 Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-operational-launch-of-x402-foundation-to-standardize-internet-native-payments-for-ai-agents-and-applications) on July 14. Coinbase contributed the x402 protocol, and the Solana Foundation joined as a founding premier member alongside payment networks, cloud providers, and commerce platforms. 40 member organizations will develop the HTTP payment standard under vendor-neutral governance.

Since its launch on Solana,[ ](https://solana.com/x402/what-is-x402)x402 has processed more than [35 million transactions and $10 million in volume](https://solana.com/x402/what-is-x402).

## **Consumer, Mobile, and Collectibles**

Solana Mobile launched[ Seeker Summer](https://solanamobile.com/blog/seeker-summer-is-here-complete-quests-earn-badges-and-explore-apps-all-summer-long), a four-round program running from July 7 through August 30. Sixteen apps receive featured quests in the Solana dApp Store, with on-device badges marking completed activity.[ Round 1 allocated 25 million SKR](https://solanacompass.com/news/solana-mobile-opens-25m-skr-claims-for-seeker-summer-round-1-participants) to participating users, with claims opening through Seed Vault Wallet on July 17. A Collector Crypt promotion offered the first 5,000 eligible buyers 20% off a Seeker and a $50 pack opening when paying through Solana Pay.

Claynosaurz [released animated episodes](https://x.com/Claynosaurz/status/2077024878583255322) on Amazon Prime Video on July 14. It extends a Solana-born property into streaming distribution.

## **DePIN**

[Helium network data published July 17](https://www.helium.com/blog/faster-connectivity-for-fast-food-restaurants) covered carrier offload at nearly 2,500 restaurant sites across 34 national brands in the United States. Those sites offloaded more than 274,000 GB of mobile data during one month.

[Nosana awarded a grant to Voight](https://www.nosana.com/blog/voight-receives-a-nosana-grant/) for infrastructure that gives production AI agents on Solana observability, identity, deployment, and discovery tools. Voight uses Nosana's distributed GPU network for model execution.

## **Network and Developer Infrastructure**

Solana activated[ 100M compute-unit blocks](https://solana.com/upgrades/100m-cu-blocks) on mainnet on July 29. SIMD-0286 raised the block limit from 60M to 100M CUs, while the 12M CU limit for a single writable account remained unchanged.

![](/uploads/posts/solana-ecosystem-roundup-july-2026/100m.webp)

Mainnet also reached[ epoch 1,000](https://solana.com/epoch1000) during July.

Core teams shipped releases throughout the month. [July also recorded](https://solana.com/changelog) new versions of Agave, Firedancer, Frankendancer, Solana Kit, Solana Go, program JavaScript SDKs, LiteSVM, Mollusk, Surfpool, Cloudbreak, and Superbank. Agave merged the final feature flag required for Alpenglow activation work.

The Solana Foundation expanded[ solana.com/data](https://solana.com/data) with near-real-time RPC comparisons across Alchemy, Helius, QuickNode, and Triton One. The dashboard places RPC-provider performance data beside Solana network, stablecoin, and DeFi metrics.

Magicblock ran [Solana Blitz v6](https://solanacompass.com/news/magicblock-solana-blitz-v6-41-mobile-apps-three-winners-one-weekend) from July 10 through July 12 with Solana Mobile and Blueshift. Forty-one teams submitted mobile apps. CapturGO placed first, followed by Perps Rider and Hunch.[ Superteam UK announced SpeedBuild London](https://x.com/SuperteamUK/status/2075202136061702423), a 12-hour agentic-finance competition with $20,000 in prizes.
