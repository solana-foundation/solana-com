The Solana Community Awards are run by the community, for the community. The people of the Solana ecosystem nominate the finalists, vote for the winners, and host the show.

## What are the Solana Community Awards?

The Solana Community Awards celebrate the people and communities that make Solana what it is: the artists, creators, traders, streamers, memers, and projects that the community nominates and votes for. The first edition took place at Breakpoint 2025 in Abu Dhabi, and this year the awards are back for round two in London.

The 2026 ceremony takes place on Friday, November 13, 2026, 7–9 PM at Somerset House, London, two days before [Breakpoint 2026](https://solana.com/breakpoint) (November 15–17, Olympia London). Join in person by [registering on Luma](https://luma.com/sol-community-awards), or watch from home on the livestream, which features videos from Solana creators. Voting closes Monday, November 9, and winners are announced live on the night.

Sponsored by [SatRush](https://x.com/SatRush), a Bitcoin mining game built on Solana where players mine and earn Bitcoin.
