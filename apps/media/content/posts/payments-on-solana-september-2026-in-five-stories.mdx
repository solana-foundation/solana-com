# Payments on Solana: September 2026 in Five Stories

In September an FDIC-insured bank put stablecoins into its core ledger, and Solana started settling a million payments a second. Here are five payments stories from the month on Solana.

## 1. Payment Channels went live, taking Solana to 1,000,000 payments per second

Every call an agent makes needs a signature approval and settlement, which gets more complicated when agents are making hundreds of small paid calls in a loop. [Payment Channels](https://solana.com/news/payment-channels-1-million-payments-per-second), live on Solana since September 3, let an agent authorize a spending limit once, spend against the limit, and settle the payment amount once. Settlement moves to the end instead of the middle. Alibaba Cloud API endpoints were live through it on day one, with no account setup and no per-call approvals.

"Think of it like a bar tab or a prepaid meter: you put money down up front, run up usage without paying for each transaction, and settle payment once when you leave."

Rishin Sharma Head of AI Growth, Solana Foundation

[In testing, 100,000 unique wallets ran through a payment channel proxy](https://solana.com/news/payment-channels-1-million-payments-per-second) issuing more than a million payments per second, enough capacity for over 80 billion payments in 24 hours at an estimated $0.000000000776 per payment.

## 2. Column built stablecoins into its banking core

Column is the FDIC-insured bank behind Brex and Slash. In September it [built stablecoins into its banking core](https://column.com/stablecoins), so USDC and USDT on Solana convert to and from dollars 24/7, instantly, with Solana as the default network. Most bank stablecoin work to date has sat beside the core ledger, in a partnership or a separate product. This sits inside it.

## 3. Open USD launched with reserves at BlackRock, Lead Bank and BNY

[OUSD went live on Solana](https://solana.com/news/open-usd-is-live-on-solana) at the end of the month, issued by Stablecoin on behalf of Open Standard, with reserves held at BlackRock, Lead Bank and BNY. Businesses can mint and burn 1:1 at no cost through BVNK, Stripe and Visa's Stablecoin Platform, which puts three of the largest distribution routes in payments on the same instrument from launch.

## 4. Solana carried 76% of all x402 agent payments

Solana took [76% of x402 transactions](https://x.com/solana/status/2102285997304148088) between August 22 and September 19, [23.2 million against 3.39 million](https://x.com/solana/status/2102285997304148088) on the next-largest network. On September 2 it processed [1.81 million agent payments in 24 hours](https://x.com/tokens/status/2095178559908380701). Nansen set a [weekly record for agent purchases of its smart money data](https://x.com/solana/status/2104729754243084553) on Solana, more than on all other networks combined, at roughly a cent per call through PayAI Network.

## 5. Stablecoin supply on Solana reached an all-time high of $17.3 billion

Supply hit [a record $17.3 billion on September 25](https://x.com/solana/status/2103484678590353515). [Daily stablecoin active addresses reached 888,000](https://x.com/solana/status/2099910897657602109) during the month, up 269% from 333,000 a year earlier. Solana carried [420 million USDC transactions in a month](https://x.com/solana/status/2100880114242511052), and [6.2 million people sent USDC on it over 30 days](https://x.com/solana/status/2097631542155870578), which is 42% of everyone sending USDC anywhere and 2.5x the next network.
