**Breakpoint returns November 15–17, 2026, bringing more than 7,000 people from 100+ countries to Olympia London.** The three-day conference brings the Solana ecosystem together for product launches, ecosystem strategy, culture, and the conversations that continue beyond the stage.

Breakpoint is designed to be experienced, not simply watched. Sessions will be available after the event, but attendees can use the week to meet builders, founders, investors, institutions, creators, and communities working across Solana.

## What to expect at Breakpoint

Breakpoint convenes people building across the ecosystem, from teams behind major integrations to people exploring where they fit. Attendees come to build, raise, invest, trade, create, and connect.

The 2026 program will examine the forces shaping the supercycle today and the technologies that may shape what comes next.

- **Day 1: The Supercycle, Today** will focus on institutional assets already moving onchain and live on Solana, including stablecoins, payments, tokenized real-world assets, and the macroeconomic forces accelerating onchain financial infrastructure.
- **Day 2: The Supercycle, Tomorrow** will explore AI agents, prediction markets, consumer applications, new forms of ownership, and markets enabled by programmable capital.

## Why London

London was selected for Breakpoint 2026 for its role as a global hub for capital markets, entrepreneurship, technology, and culture. The city offers deep institutional expertise, a global talent base, and a setting for founders and builders working on the future of finance.

Breakpoint 2025 in Abu Dhabi featured more than 75 product launches, establishing a baseline for the ecosystem’s releases and announcements when it gathers in person. Watch the [Breakpoint 2025 archive](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLilwLeBwGuK53OUOcc_GsCcbqcU5V4H9Z).

Olympia London, a Victorian exhibition hall on Hammersmith Road in West London, has hosted major events for more than a century. The venue is roughly 10 minutes from central London via the District and Circle lines and can accommodate several thousand delegates at once.

London is accessible from the Americas, Europe, and the Middle East, with transit, food, culture, and after-hours events across the city. It also provides a backdrop for discussions around payments, stablecoins, tokenized assets, institutional adoption, and onchain capital markets.

## Plan for a full week

The main conference runs November 15–17, but builders and attendees can also take part in events before and around Breakpoint. Arriving early may make room for technical programming, workshops, and community events before the main conference begins.

### Official and ecosystem events

Several events are scheduled ahead of or alongside Breakpoint, each serving a different part of the ecosystem.

- **Solana Hacker House London Edition** runs **November 1–12, 10:00–20:00**, with two weeks of building, workshops, and mentorship for developers, builders, prospective co-founders, and others looking to spend time with the Solana community. [Register for Hacker House](https://luma.com/london-hh-26).
- **200ms** takes place **November 13, 09:00–18:00** and focuses on Solana’s microstructure and decentralized finance (DeFi) landscape, including implications for DeFi projects and traders. Attendance is free but selective, with priority for Solana builders, core developers, and onchain traders. [Register for 200ms](https://luma.com/6xip72je).
- **Scale or Die** takes place **November 14, 09:30–18:30** as an application-only summit for engineers, validators, protocol developers, infrastructure teams, and researchers building on Solana. [Apply for Scale or Die](https://luma.com/scale-or-die-26).
- **BlockZero 2026** takes place **November 15, 08:30–17:00** as a community-led summit for the Solana validator ecosystem and people working closely with infrastructure. [Register for BlockZero 2026](https://luma.com/dmxdc394).

Community members will also host mixers, happy hours, dinners, demo nights, and parties throughout the week. Browse or submit an event through the [Breakpoint community events list](https://luma.com/BP-SideEvents).

## Know who you want to meet

Breakpoint brings together a broad cross-section of the ecosystem. Identifying the conversations most relevant to your goals can help you use limited time intentionally.

- **Developers and builders** include engineers as well as people building platforms, protocols, and onchain experiences across marketing, business development, project management, and community.
- **Founders** may be pitching, hiring, fundraising, or looking for integrations.
- **Institutions and policy professionals** include bankers, traditional-finance professionals, and lawyers.
- **Investors** include venture capital firms and angel investors.
- **Community members** include collectors, traders, investing communities, niche groups, activists, and daily users of the network.

There is no dress code. Attendees range from casual clothing to suits, so the priority is to arrive ready for the meetings and events most relevant to you.

## Book travel and tickets early

Flights and hotels generally become more expensive as the event approaches, while accommodation near Olympia London becomes harder to find. Booking early can reduce commute time during three days of programming and surrounding events.

If you need a visa, review the [UK visa requirements](https://www.gov.uk/check-uk-visa) as soon as possible. The Solana Foundation has engaged the Immigration Advice Service to help attendees seeking visa support; attendees are responsible for the cost of those services. [Start a priority visa application](https://iasservices.org.uk/breakpoint-priority-visa-application-centre/).

## Set one goal before you arrive

A clear goal can help attendees decide which sessions, events, and conversations deserve their attention. Rather than trying to see every talk or meet every person, define what would make the trip successful.

Possible goals include:

- Finding a team or employer in the Solana ecosystem.
- Connecting with a specific person, project, or community.
- Signing new clients or meeting potential collaboration partners.
- Bringing a remote team together and meeting its community in person.
- Learning where Solana infrastructure is heading to inform an investment thesis.
- Producing content and growing an audience.

A successful Breakpoint can mean forming a handful of meaningful relationships, gaining a clearer view of where the ecosystem is heading, and leaving with concrete follow-ups on the calendar.
