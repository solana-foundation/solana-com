**Today, [Project Harmonia](https://www.project-harmonia.com) opens its [Request for Proposals](https://particula.io/blog/project-harmonia-opens-for-applications), the program connecting Allfunds' institutional fund distribution network with Solana.**

[Allfunds](https://www.allfunds.com/en/) is the world's largest fund distribution network, connecting more than 3,300 asset managers and financial institutions, with approximately €1.9 trillion in assets under administration as of June 30, 2026. Project Harmonia makes tokenized funds distributable at institutional scale, so a fund built for one side of the market reaches the other through a single integration.

Tokenized funds have moved from pilots to production, and distribution is the last barrier. Harmonia builds a direct commercial bridge between traditional fund distribution and public decentralized finance. A tokenized fund available through Allfunds becomes accessible on Solana, and a fund live on Solana becomes distributable through Allfunds, with no separate go-to-market path to build for each. Institutional participants can explore new distribution without abandoning the operating models they already run.

The RFP is open now, on two tracks. Track A is for tokenized funds already live on Solana and ready for distribution through Allfunds today. Track B is for funds still in development, admitted on a rolling basis over the next 6 to 12 months. Issuers, distributors, and ecosystem providers supporting the tokenized fund value chain can all apply, with no admission fees. Submissions close October 24, 2026, and the first cohort is targeted to go live across the Allfunds network and Solana in Q4 2026 and Q1 2027.

## Why Solana

Fund distribution of this size needs infrastructure proven to carry real volume at low cost, and an ecosystem already home to institutional products. Solana runs live institutional tokenized funds today rather than pilots, and already hosts $4B+ in institutional real-world assets onchain.. For Allfunds, the decision came down to that scale, and to a community that understands the fund industry rather than only the technology.
