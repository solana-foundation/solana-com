AI runs on tokens. Internet capital markets run on tokens too.

The last cycle was built on software and cheap money. This one is being built out of steel, silicon, and energy: commodities, chips, data centers, the physical infrastructure that AI and modern markets now depend on. Building at that scale means moving capital as fast as the thing you're building.

Tokenization is what makes capital move that fast. It turns assets, identity, and even intelligence into programmable units that markets can price, settle, and move instantly, anywhere in the world. Solana is the capital markets and settlement layer built for exactly this, the rails financing the supercycle for everyone, everywhere.

That is the Token Supercycle, and Breakpoint is where you'll see it before the rest of the world does.

From November 15 to 17 at Olympia, Breakpoint 2026 brings builders, institutions, and policymakers together to shape what comes next.

Long before anyone tokenized anything, London, England was where global markets got priced. For most of the last century[, the world's gold price was set twice a day in a single room](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2013-11-26/gold-fix-drawing-scrutiny-amid-knowledge-tied-to-eruption), a few firms settling on the number over the phone. London is still the home of commodity trading, and it's where English common law comes from, the legal system newer financial hubs adopt when they want investors to trust them. [Kazakhstan built its entire financial center on i](https://court.aifc.kz/about-aifc-court/)t, and Abu Dhabi did the same. If this macro supercycle is about moving real assets around the world, there's no better place to make the case than the city that's been doing it longer than anywhere else.

London is giving birth to the next wave of tokenization - the city of London is building out Internet Capital Markets, and King's Cross  has emerged as [one of Europe’s most important AI hubs.](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/around-the-globe/google-europe/united-kingdom/platform-37-the-ai-exchange/) Breakpoint is where they meet.
