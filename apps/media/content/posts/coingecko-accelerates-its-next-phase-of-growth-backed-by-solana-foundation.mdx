## CoinGecko Accelerates Its Next Phase of Growth, Backed by Solana Foundation

Today at Breakpoint 2026 in London, CoinGecko announced a new chapter of growth, backed by Solana Foundation.

For more than a decade, CoinGecko has been the #1 crypto market data provider, serving 18 million registered users and more than 50 million monthly sessions, while also powering 180,000 crypto applications and businesses through their APIs. It provides reliable data on more than 50 million cryptocurrencies across over 2,000 exchanges, giving users, developers, and businesses a comprehensive view of the market.

Having helped millions of people discover and understand digital assets, CoinGecko is now focused on making it easier to participate in the digital asset economy.

What’s next for CoinGecko

With Solana Foundation’s backing, CoinGecko will accelerate its platform across three areas:

- Data & API Infrastructure: Strengthening data infrastructure, expanding coverage across onchain assets, RWAs and derivatives, and serving more developers, businesses and financial institutions.

- Market Intelligence & Discovery: Expanding market insights and creating simpler, more connected experiences to help users discover, understand and navigate crypto markets.

- Trading: Exploring new capabilities that connect crypto discovery and research with market participation.

Data integrity and community trust

CoinGecko has spent more than a decade building a trusted crypto market data platform. It will continue to use its proprietary technology and invest in its data infrastructure, expanding coverage and improving the speed, quality, and accessibility of market intelligence across the industry.

As its products evolve, CoinGecko remains committed to transparent, publicly accessible methodologies and to maintaining the data integrity and trust that users have come to expect. Its website, app, and API continue to operate as usual.

“CoinGecko is one of the most recognized and trusted platforms in crypto. The opportunity now is to build on that foundation with better products, deeper intelligence and simpler ways for people to participate. We believe the next wave of adoption will come from making crypto dramatically easier to use. Backing CoinGecko’s next chapter is about helping deliver that experience to the entire industry.” — Vibhu Norby, Chief Product Officer, Solana Foundation
