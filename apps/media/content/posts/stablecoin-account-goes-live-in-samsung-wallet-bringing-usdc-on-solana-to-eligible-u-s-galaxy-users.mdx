**Stablecoin Account went live in Samsung Wallet on Nov. 2, 2026, giving eligible Galaxy users in the United States direct access to USDC on Solana.** Users can buy, hold, send, and receive USDC in the wallet app they already use, with no separate crypto app or private keys to manage.

The feature was [first announced on Oct. 7](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/samsung-partners-with-solana-to-natively-deliver-stablecoins-in-samsung-wallet-to-82-million-us-galaxy-devices-302901200.html?tc=eml_cleartime). Stablecoin Account sits alongside the cards, boarding passes, and IDs users already keep in Samsung Wallet.

## How Stablecoin Account works

At launch, Stablecoin Account reaches approximately 82 million compatible Galaxy devices in the U.S. Transfers settle on Solana.

![Stablecoin Account in Samsung Wallet: the account card, balance and USDC send flow on Solana](/uploads/posts/stablecoin-account-goes-live-in-samsung-wallet-bringing-usdc-on-solana-to-eligible-u-s-galaxy-users/how-it-works.webp)

Samsung built the feature with Bastion, a licensed stablecoin custodian and infrastructure provider, and Coinbase, which safeguards USDC as Bastion's sub-custodian through Coinbase Prime Vault.

## Sending USDC and money

Stablecoin Account supports transfers to other Samsung Wallet users, compatible external crypto wallets, and qualifying bank accounts.

![Sending USDC abroad from Samsung Wallet's Stablecoin Account, with the amount converted to local currency](/uploads/posts/stablecoin-account-goes-live-in-samsung-wallet-bringing-usdc-on-solana-to-eligible-u-s-galaxy-users/international-transfer.webp)

- Users can send USDC to other Samsung Wallet users in the U.S. and compatible external crypto wallets at no Samsung transfer fee. Fees charged by other providers may apply.
- Eligible users can send money to qualifying bank accounts in more than 60 countries, where recipients may receive local currency. Fees and availability vary by destination.

Both options are available from the Stablecoin Account card in Samsung Wallet's “Quick access” tab: tap Send, choose a method, and follow the prompts.

## Availability

Stablecoin Account is available to U.S. residents aged 18 and over with a Samsung Account on compatible Galaxy devices running Android 13 or higher. It is not available to New York residents.

Samsung has said it expects to explore online and in-store stablecoin payments and expansion to additional markets, subject to local regulation.
