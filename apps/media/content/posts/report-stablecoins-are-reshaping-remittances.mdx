# Report: Stablecoins Are Reshaping Remittances

Remittance rails have scarcely changed in fifty years, and the cost shows up in three places: a 6.49% average fee on a $200 transfer, three to five business days to settle, and hundreds of billions of dollars locked in pre-funded accounts to guarantee it arrives on time.

[An Evolution of Money Movement: How Stablecoins on Solana Are Reshaping Remittances](https://solana.com/reports/remittances-report) is a new report from Solana Foundation, written for established money transfer operators and fintech startups evaluating what to do about it.

## What the report finds

* Transfers are still treated as dead ends. There is no additive service such as a savings account, credit score, investment access, or financial identity attached to it
* The market still ignores 1.3 billion unbanked adults, even though nearly half of them carry smartphones
* Sending remittances using stablecoins settles nearly instantly, is highly cost-efficient, and reaches the unbanked

## Who is already building it

[Western Union](https://www.westernunion.com) launched USDPT, issued through [Anchorage Digital Bank](https://www.anchorage.com), alongside its Digital Asset Network and Stablecard with [Rain](https://www.rain.xyz). [Zepz](https://zepz.io), the company behind [WorldRemit](https://www.worldremit.com) and [Sendwave](https://www.sendwave.com), launched the Sendwave Wallet for recipients across more than 100 countries. [Tala](https://tala.co), which serves 13 million customers in emerging markets, deployed a $50 million tokenized lending facility with [Huma Finance](https://huma.finance).

Underneath them, [Yellow Card](https://yellowcard.io), [Flutterwave](https://flutterwave.com), [Bitso](https://bitso.com), [Trace Finance](https://tracefinance.com), and [Sphere Pay](https://spherepay.co) handle local conversion in the corridors where the money lands.

## What's inside

* The composable stack, with the Solana projects deployed at each layer
* Five business models beyond money movement, from wallets as financial platforms to stablecoin issuance as revenue
* Four entry paths, from low-risk treasury optimization to launching a full stablecoin platform, each with its barrier to entry, time to value, and revenue model
* Corridor deep dives for the U.S. to Mexico and Brazil, Nigeria, the Philippines, and India, with the flows, the partners, and the regulations in each market
* Regulatory detail and risks, market by market

The first generation of stablecoin-powered remittance products is here.
