**Wall Street's core infrastructure is heading to Solana.**

The Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation (DTCC), the post-trade market infrastructure behind the global financial system, announced plans to enable the tokenization of eligible DTC-custodied assets on Solana. The move connects the DTCC Tokenization Service (DTS) to Solana, giving market participants a direct path between regulated market infrastructure and a public network already running financial activity at scale.

DTCC is the premier post-trade market infrastructure for the global financial services industry. In 2025, its subsidiaries processed securities transactions valued at $4.7 quadrillion, and its depository provided custody and asset servicing for securities from more than 150 countries valued at $114 trillion. Bringing that infrastructure onchain gives institutions a regulated route to issue, settle, and mobilize tokenized assets without leaving the protections of traditional markets.

Here is what the integration involves:

- The DTCC Tokenization Service will connect to Solana in the second half of 2027.
- Eligible traditional assets can then be converted into tokenized form, with full token lifecycle functionality.
- DTC-tokenized assets keep the same investor protections, ownership rights and regulatory safeguards as traditionally held securities.

For institutions, the same assets gain liquidity, collateral mobility, and capital efficiency of an always-on network while retaining the legal and regulatory standing they have today.

## Why Solana

Institutions are already issuing securities, settling transactions, and deploying capital on Solana. Its sub-second finality and low fees let tokenized securities settle and move as easily as any other asset onchain, around the clock. Connecting DTS to Solana adds regulated, DTC-tokenized securities to a network built for continuous markets, rather than one constrained by traditional settlement windows.

> "Institutions chose Solana because it already runs real financial activity at scale. Bringing the DTCC Tokenization Service to Solana connects one of the most trusted names in post-trade infrastructure with a network built for always-on markets, enabling tokenized securities to settle and move as easily as any other asset onchain." – Nick Ducoff, GM, Institutional, Solana Foundation
