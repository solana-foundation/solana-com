Every change on Solana is a transaction: a list of instructions, signed by the
accounts they spend from. In this step you send 1 USDC from your wallet to a
new address. It settles in under a second, and the fee is a fraction of a cent,
paid in SOL.

## Send the transaction

Save this as `send-dollars.ts` next to `client.ts` and run
`npx tsx send-dollars.ts`.

<Send />

The script prints a link to your transaction in Solana Explorer.

What the code does:

- `tokenBalance(...)`, from `client.ts`, checks that you have at least 1 USDC
  before sending.
- `client.token.instructions.transferToATA(...)` builds two instructions:
  1. Open the recipient's USDC token account if it doesn't exist (and do
     nothing if it does). Your wallet pays its small deposit.
  2. Move 1 USDC: 1,000,000 base units, because USDC has 6 decimals. As a
     `TransferChecked` instruction, it also checks the mint and decimals, so a
     wrong amount fails instead of sending 1,000 times too much.
- `.sendTransaction()` puts both in one transaction, with your wallet as fee
  payer, signs and sends it, and returns its signature once confirmed. The
  account is never opened without the payment arriving.

<Tip>
**No devnet USDC yet?** The script says so instead of sending. Get test USDC
as in [step 1](/developers/docs/quickstart/check-balance#get-test-dollars),
then run `npx tsx send-dollars.ts` again.
</Tip>
