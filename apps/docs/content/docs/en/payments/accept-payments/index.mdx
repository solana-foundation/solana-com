## Receiving Address Convention

On Solana, **share your wallet address, not your token account**. The sender
derives your [associated token account](/developers/docs/payments/how-payments-work#token-accounts)
for whatever token they send.

**Example:** Share `7EcDhS...` (your wallet), not `9xKpQ...` (your USDC token
account).

## Integration Options

| Approach                                                            | Best For                      | Complexity |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------- |
| [PaymentButton](/developers/docs/tools/commerce-kit/quickstart/payment-button) | React apps, quick integration | Low        |
| [Pay.sh](/developers/docs/payments/pay-sh)                                     | Paid APIs and subscriptions   | Low        |
| [Solana Pay](/developers/docs/tools/solana-pay)                                | QR codes, payment links, POS  | Medium     |
| [Direct RPC](/developers/docs/payments/interacting-with-solana)                | Custom flows, full control    | Higher     |

## Verification

Verify every payment on your server before you fulfil the order.
[Confirm payments from your backend](/developers/docs/payments/checkout/backend) shows the
checks, [Verification tools](/developers/docs/payments/accept-payments/verification-tools)
covers balances and transaction history, and
[Indexing](/developers/docs/payments/accept-payments/indexing) covers high-volume
monitoring.
