This tutorial builds a checkout that takes a 1 USDC payment and confirms it from
your own server, in four steps and about 30 minutes. Steps 1 and 2 run in your
browser, including a payment button you can try with test money, so there's
nothing to install until step 3.

## How a payment works

1. **Request.** Your checkout creates an order with an amount and a unique
   reference.
2. **Approve.** The customer's
   <span className="sol-chip" data-concept="wallet"><Tooltip headline="Wallets & signatures" tip="A wallet holds a key pair: a public address that anyone can pay, and a private key that signs approvals and never leaves the device." cta="Learn more" href="/developers/docs/core/wallets">wallet</Tooltip><span className="nojs-only"> ([Wallets & signatures](/developers/docs/core/wallets): A wallet holds a key pair: a public address that anyone can pay, and a private key that signs approvals and never leaves the device.)</span></span> signs one
   USDC transfer to your store.
3. **Settle.** The network confirms the
   <span className="sol-chip" data-concept="transaction"><Tooltip headline="Transactions" tip="A transaction is a signed request to change the shared ledger. It either fully happens or doesn't happen at all, and once confirmed anyone can look it up." cta="Learn more" href="/developers/docs/core/transactions">transaction</Tooltip><span className="nojs-only"> ([Transactions](/developers/docs/core/transactions): A transaction is a signed request to change the shared ledger. It either fully happens or doesn't happen at all, and once confirmed anyone can look it up.)</span></span> usually
   in under a second.
4. **Verify.** Your server looks the transaction up by its signature and checks
   the amount, the recipient, and the order reference.
5. **Ship.** You fulfil the order.

## Is this the right tool?

Most teams compare this with cards. Here is that comparison for a \$20 sale:

| | Cards | Bank transfer | USDC on Solana |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fee | about \$0.88 (1) | \$0 to \$30 | about \$0.0005 (2) |
| Money reaches you | about 2 business days | 1 to 5 days | under 1 s (3) |
| Chargebacks | Yes | Rare | No (4) |
| Across borders | Extra FX fees | Slow, costly | As domestic |
| Customer needs | A card | A bank account | A USDC wallet |

1. Cards through Stripe at its published standard rate for domestic cards,
   2.9% + 30¢ ([stripe.com/pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing), checked 13
   Oct 2026).
2. A flat network fee of 5,000 lamports per transaction, whatever the amount
   (about \$0.0005 at \$100/SOL).
3. The USDC is in your wallet in under a second. Cashing it out to a bank
   account goes through an off-ramp and takes longer.
4. A refund is a payment you send back.

<Columns cols={2}>
<Card title="A good fit if" icon="check" color="#16a34a">
- You sell across borders, or to people without cards
- Your margins or ticket sizes are small
- You want instant payouts or automatic revenue splits

- You haven't checked how your jurisdiction treats stablecoins
</Card>
