<Callout type="info" title="Summary">

Each option below adds confidentiality or operational control, with less
composability and more integration work.
[Privacy on Solana](/docs/finance/privacy) compares the privacy models
themselves. The sections below take each one from the issuer's side.

</Callout>

## The options

Ordered by how far each takes the asset from the transparent public network.
Stop at the first option that meets your requirement.

| Option                                                                 | Provides                                                                                                         | Considerations                                                            | Applies when                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Transparent mainnet                                                    | The full public network: wallet, custodian and venue support, and atomic settlement against other onchain assets | Balances, counterparties, and amounts are readable                        | Holdings and flows are not confidential                                           |
| [Confidential Balances](/docs/tokens/extensions/confidential-transfer) | Encrypted balances and transfer amounts on the same mint                                                         | Amount-dependent policy stops composing; adoption is still limited        | Position sizes or payment amounts are commercially sensitive                      |
| [Solana Privacy Rings](https://www.helius.dev/docs/privacy/)           | Programmable privacy; participant anonymity only in a Custom Ring                                                | In beta on devnet at the time of writing; integration is through its APIs | Policy and privacy are needed together, or sender anonymity (Custom Ring)         |
| [Private Channels](/docs/tools/private-channels)                       | Private execution inside a perimeter the institution operates                                                    | Shared liquidity, composability, and existing venue and custodian support | Operational control over participation and ordering is required, not only privacy |

## Transparent mainnet

The default. An ordinary Token-2022 mint on the public network, with the control
surface from
[choosing token controls](/docs/tokenization/design/choosing-controls).

- **Provides** the wallet, custodian, venue, indexer, and oracle support
  available to Token-2022 mints with the chosen extensions, and atomic
  settlement against other assets on the same ledger without a bridge.
- **Considerations**: no confidentiality; balances, counterparties, and amounts
  are all readable.
- **Applies when** the asset's holdings and flows are not confidential, or
  counsel has confirmed the obligation is met notwithstanding that addresses are
  pseudonymous rather than anonymous.

## Confidential Balances

The same mint with the
[confidential transfer extension](/docs/tokens/extensions/confidential-transfer)
enabled.

- **Private**: token balances and confidential transfer amounts.
- **Public**: the mint, token accounts, account owners, and transaction
  participation.
- **Audit**: an optional ElGamal auditor key can decrypt transfer amounts,
  without revealing a full balance or granting authority to move tokens.
- **Controls**: freeze, default-frozen accounts, and pause compose with
  encrypted balances; a permanent delegate reaches only the public balance;
  amount-dependent rules do not compose.

Confidential Balances depends on the ZK ElGamal Proof Program; confirm it is
enabled on the target cluster before building against it. Confirm support with
the venues and custodians the asset depends on. The control qualification is
worked through in
[privacy and transfer controls](/docs/tokenization/compliance/privacy-and-transfer-controls).

**This option does not conceal the transaction graph.** Who transacted with whom
stays public. If concealing participation is the requirement, a Custom Ring or a
private channel addresses it.

## Solana Privacy Rings

Encrypted onchain balances and programmable privacy through APIs, documented by
Helius as Solana Rings. In beta on devnet at the time of writing.

- **Default Ring** is permissionless and confidential: the asset and amount are
  private, while sender and recipient stay public.
- **Custom Rings** are Solana programs that can be confidential or anonymous,
  and can carry transfer policy, reporting, compliance controls, and auditor
  visibility.
- **Settlement** is self-custodial, can move between Rings, and settles natively
  on Solana in one transaction.

For an issuance this is the option where policy and privacy are expressed in the
same place rather than split between the mint and an off-chain check. Treat the
beta status as a gating question for a production issuance, and confirm the
custody and venue path before committing.
[Read the Solana Privacy Rings documentation](https://www.helius.dev/docs/privacy/).

## Private Channels

Private execution infrastructure for institutions that need operational control
in addition to transaction privacy.

- **Inside the channel** transfers are private and confirm within the channel,
  with no set per-transaction fee.
- **The operator** defines participation, access controls, ordering, rate
  limits, compliance requirements, and data visibility.
- **Audit** is by policy: external observers cannot inspect channel activity,
  while the institution can grant auditors the visibility it requires.
- **Mainnet relationship**: value moves into and out of the channel through
  Solana mainnet, while execution inside the channel stays private.

This is the only option that leaves the shared execution environment, so it
gives up shared liquidity, composability with other assets, and the wallet,
venue, and custodian support an asset has on mainnet.
[Explore Private Channels](/docs/tools/private-channels).

<Callout type="info" title="Control without leaving mainnet">

A preference for greater control does not require moving the asset off the
public network. The token-layer controls in
[choosing token controls](/docs/tokenization/design/choosing-controls) provide
much of it while keeping interoperability.

</Callout>

## Factors that change the order

Two findings override the order above. A venue or custodian the asset depends on
that does not support Token-2022 with the intended extensions constrains every
option, so confirm that first - see
[program and authority design](/docs/tokenization/design/program-and-authority-design).
A requirement for participant anonymity, or for amount-dependent policy
alongside amount confidentiality, rules out Confidential Balances and points to
a Custom Ring or a private channel - see
[privacy and transfer controls](/docs/tokenization/compliance/privacy-and-transfer-controls).
