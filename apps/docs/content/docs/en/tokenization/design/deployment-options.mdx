<Callout type="info" title="Summary">

Start at mainnet and move away from it only when a requirement you can name
forces you to. Each step away buys confidentiality and pays for it in
composability and integration work. Most issuance requirements are met by one of
the two options below.

</Callout>

## Start at mainnet

The options are ordered by how much they take you away from the public network.
Each step is a cost accepted to meet a requirement you can name. Work down this
list and stop at the first option that meets your requirement.

If your question is what a given privacy model conceals,
[Privacy on Solana](/docs/finance/privacy) is the authoritative comparison.

## Option 1 - Mainnet, transparent

The default. Your token is an ordinary Token-2022 mint on the public network,
with the control surface from
[choosing controls](/docs/tokenization/design/choosing-controls).

You get the whole ecosystem: every wallet, custodian, venue, indexer, and
oracle, plus atomic settlement against any other asset on the same ledger
without a bridge. You also get a public ledger, where balances, counterparties,
and amounts are all readable.

**Stay here if** your asset's holdings and flows are not confidential, or you
have confirmed with counsel that the obligation is met notwithstanding that
addresses are pseudonymous rather than anonymous.

## Option 2 - Mainnet with Confidential Balances

The same mint with the
[confidential transfer extension](/docs/tokens/extensions/confidential-transfer)
enabled. [Privacy on Solana](/docs/finance/privacy) sets out what Confidential
Balances hides and what it leaves public.

The proof system these depend on is enabled on mainnet-beta, so this option is
deployable. Adoption remains limited, and account-level controls compose cleanly
here while any amount-dependent rule does not. Both qualifications are covered
in
[privacy and transfer controls](/docs/tokenization/compliance/privacy-and-transfer-controls).

**Move here if** position sizes or payment amounts are commercially sensitive or
confidential.

**This option does not conceal the transaction graph.** Concealing who
transacted with whom is not addressed by the released functionality documented
here. If that is your requirement, treat it as an open question and start from
the model comparison in [Privacy on Solana](/docs/finance/privacy).

<Callout type="info" title="Wanting more control is not by itself a reason to leave mainnet">

A preference for greater control does not require moving the asset off the
public network. The token-layer controls in
[choosing controls](/docs/tokenization/design/choosing-controls) provide much of
it without giving up interoperability - and anything that leaves mainnet gives
up shared liquidity, composability with other assets, and existing custodian and
venue support.

</Callout>

## What would change the recommendation

- **A venue or custodian you depend on does not support Token-2022 with your
  extensions.** This constrains both options and is worth confirming before
  anything else - see
  [program and authority design](/docs/tokenization/design/program-and-authority-design).
- **Your requirement turns out to be amount-dependent policy plus
  confidentiality.** Neither option resolves that conflict onchain; see
  [privacy and transfer controls](/docs/tokenization/compliance/privacy-and-transfer-controls).
