<Info>
Each option after public mainnet adds confidentiality or operational control,
reduces composability, and adds to what integrators need to support.
[Private payments](/developers/docs/finance/privacy) compares the privacy
solutions themselves; this page takes each one from the issuer's side.
Confidential Balances is available on mainnet. Solana Privacy Rings and Private
Channels are coming, but are not yet ready for production.
</Info>

## The options

Listed from fully public to most private.

| Option                                                                           | Provides                                                                                                    | Considerations                                                                                                       | Applies when                                                                       | Availability                          |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Public mainnet                                                                   | Wallet, custodian, and venue support, and atomic settlement against other onchain assets                    | Balances, counterparties, and amounts are readable                                                                   | Holdings and flows are not confidential                                            | Available                             |
| [Confidential Balances](/developers/docs/tokens/extensions/confidential-transfer) | Encrypted balances and transfer amounts on the same mint                                                    | Policy that depends on amounts stops working onchain; wallet, custodian, and venue support varies                    | Position sizes or payment amounts are commercially sensitive                       | Available on mainnet                  |
| Solana Privacy Rings                                                             | Private asset and amount; private sender and recipient in a Custom Ring                                     | Integration is through Helius's APIs                                                                                 | Policy and privacy are needed together, or sender anonymity                        | Coming soon: in beta, live on devnet  |
| [Private Channels](/developers/docs/tools/private-channels)                       | Payments inside a channel the institution operates, hidden from outside observers                          | Runs outside the shared execution environment; value moves in and out through Solana mainnet                         | Operational control over participation and ordering is required, not only privacy | Coming soon: not yet security audited |

## Public mainnet

The default: an ordinary Token-2022 mint on the public network, with the
controls from
[choosing token controls](/developers/docs/tokenization/design/choosing-controls).

It applies where holdings need not be confidential, or where counsel confirms
that pseudonymous addresses meet the confidentiality obligation.

## Confidential Balances

The same mint with the
[Confidential Balances extension](/developers/docs/tokens/extensions/confidential-transfer)
enabled. Balances and transfer amounts are encrypted; the mint, the accounts,
their owners, and which accounts transact stay public, so it does not meet a
requirement to hide who transacts. An optional auditor key can decrypt transfer
amounts.
[Privacy and transfer controls](/developers/docs/tokenization/compliance/privacy-and-transfer-controls)
covers which issuer controls still work, the decisions fixed at mint creation,
and how the auditor key is managed. Confirm that the venues and custodians the
asset depends on support the extension.

## Solana Privacy Rings

Coming soon. Solana Privacy Rings provide encrypted onchain balances and
private transfers through Helius's APIs. They are in beta and not yet ready for
production: Helius describes them as live on devnet and undergoing independent
security audits and formal verification.

- **Default Ring**: permissionless; the asset and amount are private, while the
  sender and recipient stay public.
- **Custom Rings**: add policy and auditor controls, and can also hide the
  sender and recipient.

Check the current status before planning around them, and confirm custody and
venue support.
[Read the Solana Privacy Rings documentation](https://www.helius.dev/docs/privacy/).

## Private Channels

Coming soon. Private Channels is private payment infrastructure that an
institution runs: users deposit tokens into an escrow program on mainnet, pay
each other inside the channel, and withdraw back to mainnet. Payments inside
the channel are not recorded on mainnet, so outside observers can't see them.
The institution controls who participates, what rules apply, and how data is
accessed. It has not been security audited and is not recommended for
production use with real funds.

It is the only option that runs outside the shared execution environment.
Shared liquidity, other assets, and mainnet wallet, venue, and custodian
support are reached by moving value through Solana mainnet.
[Explore Private Channels](/developers/docs/tools/private-channels).
