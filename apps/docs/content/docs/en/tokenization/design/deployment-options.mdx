<Callout type="info" title="Summary">

Each option after transparent mainnet adds confidentiality or operational
control, reduces composability, and adds to what integrators need to support.
[Privacy on Solana](/docs/finance/privacy) compares the privacy models
themselves. The sections below take each one from the issuer's side.

</Callout>

## The options

Listed from fully public to most private.

| Option                                                                 | Provides                                                                                                         | Considerations                                                                                                                                                                                            | Applies when                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Transparent mainnet                                                    | The full public network: wallet, custodian and venue support, and atomic settlement against other onchain assets | Balances, counterparties, and amounts are readable                                                                                                                                                        | Holdings and flows are not confidential                                           |
| [Confidential Balances](/docs/tokens/extensions/confidential-transfer) | Encrypted balances and transfer amounts on the same mint                                                         | Amount-dependent policy stops composing; wallet, custodian, and venue support varies; confirm per counterparty                                                                                            | Position sizes or payment amounts are commercially sensitive                      |
| [Solana Privacy Rings](https://www.helius.dev/docs/privacy/)           | Programmable privacy; participant anonymity only in a Custom Ring                                                | In beta at the time of writing; integration is through its APIs                                                                                                                                           | Policy and privacy are needed together, or sender anonymity (Custom Ring)         |
| [Private Channels](/docs/tools/private-channels)                       | Private execution inside a perimeter the institution operates                                                    | Runs in a perimeter the institution operates, outside the shared execution environment; value moves into and out of the channel through Solana mainnet; mainnet deployment pending at the time of writing | Operational control over participation and ordering is required, not only privacy |

## Transparent mainnet

The default. An ordinary Token-2022 mint on the public network, with the
controls from
[choosing token controls](/docs/tokenization/design/choosing-controls).

Applies where holdings need not be confidential, or counsel confirms
pseudonymous addresses meet the confidentiality obligation.

## Confidential Balances

The same mint with the
[confidential transfer extension](/docs/tokens/extensions/confidential-transfer)
enabled.

- **Private**: token balances and confidential transfer amounts.
- **Public**: the mint, token accounts, account owners, and transaction
  participation.
- **Audit**: an optional ElGamal auditor key can decrypt transfer amounts,
  without revealing a full balance or granting authority to move tokens.
- **Controls**: freeze, default-frozen accounts, and pause compose with
  encrypted balances; a permanent delegate reaches only the public balance;
  amount-dependent rules do not compose.

Confidential Balances depends on the ZK ElGamal Proof Program; confirm it is
enabled on the target cluster, and that the venues and custodians the asset
depends on support the extension.
[Privacy and transfer controls](/docs/tokenization/compliance/privacy-and-transfer-controls)
covers which controls still apply.

If concealing participation is the requirement, a Custom Ring or a private
channel addresses it.

## Solana Privacy Rings

Encrypted onchain balances and programmable privacy through APIs, documented by
Helius as Solana Rings. In beta at the time of writing.

- **Default Ring** is permissionless and confidential: the asset and amount are
  private, while sender and recipient stay public.
- **Custom Rings** are Solana programs that can be confidential or anonymous,
  with configurable policy, compliance controls, and auditor visibility.
- **Settlement**: transfers execute natively on Solana, can move between Rings,
  and settle in one transaction; balances are self-custodial.

For an issuance, this option expresses policy and privacy in one place. Check
the current status in the documentation linked below, and confirm custody and
venue support.
[Read the Solana Privacy Rings documentation](https://www.helius.dev/docs/privacy/)
or [try the demo](https://helius.dev/privacy/demo).

## Private Channels

Private execution infrastructure for institutions that need operational control
in addition to transaction privacy.

- **Inside the channel** transfers are private and confirm within the channel,
  with no set per-transaction fee.
- **The operator** defines participation, access controls, ordering, rate
  limits, compliance requirements, and data visibility.
- **Audit** is by policy: external observers cannot inspect channel activity,
  while the institution can grant auditors the visibility it requires.

Mainnet deployment is pending at the time of writing.

This is the only option that runs outside the shared execution environment.
Shared liquidity, other assets, and mainnet wallet, venue, and custodian support
are reached by moving value through Solana mainnet.
[Explore Private Channels](/docs/tools/private-channels).
