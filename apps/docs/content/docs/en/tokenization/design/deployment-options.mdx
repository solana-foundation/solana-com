<Callout type="info" title="Summary">

Start on transparent mainnet and move only when a requirement you can name
forces you to. Each option below buys confidentiality or operational control and
pays for it in composability and integration work.
[Privacy on Solana](/docs/finance/privacy) is the authoritative comparison of
the privacy models themselves; this page covers what each one means for an
issuance.

</Callout>

## The options

Ordered by how far each takes the asset from the transparent public network.
Stop at the first option that meets your requirement.

| Option                                                                 | What it adds                                                    | What it costs                                                             | Choose it when                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Transparent mainnet                                                    | Nothing - the full public network                               | Balances, counterparties, and amounts are readable                        | Holdings and flows are not confidential                                        |
| [Confidential Balances](/docs/tokens/extensions/confidential-transfer) | Encrypted balances and transfer amounts on the same mint        | Amount-dependent policy stops composing; adoption is still limited        | Position sizes or payment amounts are commercially sensitive                   |
| [Solana Privacy Protocol](https://www.helius.dev/docs/privacy/)        | Programmable privacy, including participant anonymity in a Ring | Beta, and integration is through its APIs rather than the mint alone      | You need policy and privacy expressed together, or sender anonymity            |
| [Private Channels](/docs/tools/private-channels)                       | Private execution inside a perimeter the institution operates   | Shared liquidity, composability, and existing venue and custodian support | You need operational control over participation and ordering, not only privacy |

## Transparent mainnet

The default. An ordinary Token-2022 mint on the public network, with the control
surface from
[choosing token controls](/docs/tokenization/design/choosing-controls).

- **You get** every wallet, custodian, venue, indexer, and oracle, plus atomic
  settlement against any other asset on the same ledger without a bridge.
- **You give up** confidentiality: balances, counterparties, and amounts are all
  readable.
- **Stay here if** the asset's holdings and flows are not confidential, or
  counsel has confirmed the obligation is met notwithstanding that addresses are
  pseudonymous rather than anonymous.

## Confidential Balances

The same mint with the
[confidential transfer extension](/docs/tokens/extensions/confidential-transfer)
enabled.

- **Private**: token balances and confidential transfer amounts.
- **Public**: the mint, token accounts, account owners, and transaction
  participation.
- **Audit**: an optional ElGamal auditor key can decrypt transfer amounts,
  without revealing a full balance or granting authority to move tokens.
- **Controls**: account-level controls compose cleanly; any amount-dependent
  rule does not.

Confidential Balances depends on the ZK ElGamal Proof Program, which is enabled
on mainnet-beta, so this option is deployable today. Adoption remains limited,
so confirm support with the venues and custodians you need. The control
qualification is worked through in
[privacy and transfer controls](/docs/tokenization/compliance/privacy-and-transfer-controls).

**This option does not conceal the transaction graph.** Who transacted with whom
stays public. If concealing participation is the requirement, the next two
options address it and this one does not.

## Solana Privacy Protocol

Encrypted onchain balances and programmable privacy through APIs, documented by
Helius as Solana Rings. Currently in beta.

- **Default Ring** is permissionless and confidential: the asset and amount are
  private, while sender and recipient stay public.
- **Custom Rings** are Solana programs that can be confidential or anonymous,
  and can carry transfer policy, reporting, compliance controls, and auditor
  visibility.
- **Settlement** is self-custodial, can move between Rings, and settles natively
  on Solana in one transaction.

For an issuance this is the option where policy and privacy are expressed in the
same place rather than split between the mint and an off-chain check. Treat the
beta status as a gating question for a production issuance, and confirm the
custody and venue path before committing.
[Read the Solana Privacy Protocol documentation](https://www.helius.dev/docs/privacy/).

## Private Channels

Private execution infrastructure for institutions that need operational control
in addition to transaction privacy.

- **Inside the channel** transfers are private and instant, with no set
  per-transaction fee.
- **The operator** defines participation, access controls, ordering, rate
  limits, compliance requirements, and data visibility.
- **Audit** is by policy: external observers cannot inspect channel activity,
  while the institution can grant auditors the visibility it requires.
- **Mainnet relationship**: value moves into and out of the channel through
  Solana mainnet, while execution inside the channel stays private.

This is the only option that leaves the shared execution environment, so it is
also the one that gives up the most: shared liquidity, composability with other
assets, and the wallet, venue, and custodian support an asset gets for free on
mainnet. [Explore Private Channels](/docs/tools/private-channels).

<Callout type="info" title="Wanting more control is not by itself a reason to leave mainnet">

A preference for greater control does not require moving the asset off the
public network. The token-layer controls in
[choosing token controls](/docs/tokenization/design/choosing-controls) provide
much of it while keeping interoperability.

</Callout>

## What would change the recommendation

| Finding                                                                             | What it means                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A venue or custodian you depend on does not support Token-2022 with your extensions | Constrains every option. Confirm this before anything else - see [program and authority design](/docs/tokenization/design/program-and-authority-design) |
| Your requirement is amount-dependent policy plus amount confidentiality             | Confidential Balances does not resolve that conflict; see [privacy and transfer controls](/docs/tokenization/compliance/privacy-and-transfer-controls)  |
| Your requirement is participant anonymity                                           | Confidential Balances leaves participation public; a Custom Ring or a private channel is the path                                                       |
| You need to operate the participation and ordering rules yourself                   | Only Private Channels offers this, at the cost of mainnet composability                                                                                 |
