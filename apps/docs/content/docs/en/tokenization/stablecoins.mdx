A stablecoin is a token redeemable for a reference currency. The token is the
issuer's liability and the reserve backing it is held separately, so the mint
is the register of record: in
[token design](/developers/docs/tokenization/design#is-the-token-the-asset-or-a-record-of-one)
terms, the token is the asset.

## Issuer and holders

Supply changes only through the issuer. Tokens are minted when reserve funds
arrive and burned when they are redeemed, and minting and redemption are
usually limited to the issuer's onboarded customers. Once issued, the token
often circulates more widely, through wallets, venues, and payment flows.

The regime the issuer operates under sets the reserve, redemption, and
disclosure rules, and regimes may differ by jurisdiction, including on whether
yield may be paid or passed through.

## The controls that matter most

- **A blocklist or an allowlist.** A broadly circulating stablecoin usually
  has to exclude specific addresses rather than vet every holder, which is the
  [blocklist](/developers/docs/tokenization/design/choosing-controls#allowlist-or-blocklist)
  model. A stablecoin limited to the issuer's own customers uses an allowlist.
- **Freeze and pause.** Freeze acts on one holder, for example after a
  sanctions match. Pause halts the whole token during an incident, so its
  authority has to be reachable at any hour.
- **A mint authority behind a quorum.** New supply is new liability; see
  [program and authority design](/developers/docs/tokenization/design/program-and-authority-design#an-onchain-multisig).
- **Little or no per-transfer code.** A stablecoin is often the cash leg of
  someone else's payment or trade. A transfer hook adds accounts and compute to
  every transfer, and venues that do not support hooks cannot settle the token;
  see
  [mint controls and the cash leg](/developers/docs/defi/cash-leg#mint-controls-and-the-cash-leg).
- **Confidential Balances**, where payment amounts are commercially sensitive.
  Rules that depend on the amount then cannot be enforced onchain; see
  [privacy and transfer controls](/developers/docs/tokenization/compliance/privacy-and-transfer-controls).

## Operations

- **Supply against the reserve.** Reconcile total supply against the reserve
  on a fixed cadence. Evidence of the reserve sits outside the mint, so publish
  it in a form that can be checked independently.
- **Payments.** [Payments](/developers/docs/payments) covers accepting,
  sending, and reconciling stablecoin payments.
- **Settlement against other assets.**
  [Choosing the cash leg](/developers/docs/defi/cash-leg) compares a
  stablecoin with a tokenized deposit and an offchain rail.

To build the mint, follow
[Issue a gated token](/developers/docs/tokenization/tutorials/issuing).
