<Callout type="info" title="Summary">

Settlement has two legs: the asset and the cash. Decide which cash instrument
carries the cash leg, decide whether both legs must move in the same
transaction, and decide which commitment level your books treat as settled. The
first two interact: the cash instrument constrains the controls on the mint, and
settling both legs in one transaction requires both to be on the same ledger.

</Callout>

## Payment, settlement, and finality

- **Payment execution** - moving value from one party to another. Documented in
  [Payments](/docs/payments).
- **Settlement** - discharging both sides of an obligation, so the asset and the
  cash both end up where they belong.
- **Finality** - the point at which the ledger will not reverse the transfer,
  which is a different question from whether your records call it settled.

## The decisions

1. **[Choose the cash leg](/docs/tokenization/settlement/cash-leg)** - a
   stablecoin, a tokenized deposit, or an off-ledger rail. The cash instrument
   and the mint's controls limit each other, so decide them together.
2. **Delivery versus payment** - exchanging the asset against its payment in a
   single transaction, so neither side is exposed to the other having failed to
   deliver. Only available when both legs are on the same ledger. The
   [DvP program](/docs/tokenization/settlement/dvp-program) holds both legs in
   escrow and lets a settlement authority settle them; the
   [extensions-only pattern](/docs/tokenization/settlement/dvp) does the same
   through delegated authority without a program.
3. **[Settlement and finality](/docs/tokenization/settlement/finality)** - what
   `processed`, `confirmed`, and `finalized` mean, how many slots each takes,
   and which one a system of record should trust.

## Off-ledger settlement

If the cash leg settles off-ledger, the two legs settle separately: the asset
leg onchain and the cash leg on the existing rail, matched by reconciliation.
One leg can complete while the other does not.
[Choose the cash leg](/docs/tokenization/settlement/cash-leg) works through what
each option requires.
