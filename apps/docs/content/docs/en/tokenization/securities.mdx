These pages treat a tokenized security as an instrument with a register of
record, a transfer agent or equivalent registrar, and holders restricted by
rule.

## Register and transfer agent

The first decision is whether the mint is the register or mirrors one held
offchain, and it is fixed at creation; see
[is the token the asset, or a record of one?](/developers/docs/tokenization/design#is-the-token-the-asset-or-a-record-of-one)
Tokenized equities commonly use the mirror, with the shares held by a custodian
and the token tracking them. A mirror brings a standing reconciliation between
two books and evidence that the underlying exists.

Either way, the transfer agent keeps the duties that are not the record of
balances: eligibility, corporate actions, holder servicing, and statutory
reporting. Onchain, that becomes a key that adds holders to the allowlist or
co-signs mint and burn; see
[transfer agent responsibilities](/developers/docs/tokenization/custody/transfer-agent-responsibilities).

## The controls that matter most

- **A holder allowlist**, with eligibility such as accreditation evidenced by
  an [attestation](/developers/docs/tokenization/compliance/attestation-based-compliance).
- **A transfer hook, only where the rule depends on the transfer.** Lockups
  that expire per tranche, jurisdiction-dependent limits on transfer size, and
  holder counts under a statutory cap cannot be expressed as a list of
  addresses. A hook runs on every transfer and narrows venue support; see
  [when a hook is required](/developers/docs/tokenization/design/choosing-controls#when-a-hook-is-required).
- **Forced transfer** through a permanent delegate, for court orders, fraud
  response, and lost-key recovery, held behind a
  [multisig](/developers/docs/tokenization/design/program-and-authority-design#an-onchain-multisig).
- **Metadata** for instrument reference data.

## Corporate actions

The issuer declares a corporate action and the transfer agent executes it.
Splits and some distributions can use
[Scaled UI Amount](/developers/docs/tokens/extensions/scaled-ui-amount), which
changes the displayed balance without a transfer, so reconcile raw amounts.
Redemptions and retirements that need the issuer's co-signature use
[permissioned burn](/developers/docs/tokens/extensions/permissioned-burn).

## Delivery versus payment

The security and its cash leg can settle in one transaction, through the
[DvP program](/developers/docs/defi/dvp-program) or through
[delegated authority](/developers/docs/tokenization/dvp). The DvP program does
not accept a mint with Scaled UI Amount, and the `tokenized-security` template
in Mosaic always adds it, so decide how trades will settle before you create
the mint. Where an offchain register governs, updating it is a separate step
from the onchain exchange; see
[finality and the register of record](/developers/docs/defi/finality#finality-and-the-register-of-record).

To build the mint, follow
[Issue a gated token](/developers/docs/tokenization/tutorials/issuing).
