These pages treat a tokenized security as an instrument with a register of
record, a transfer agent or equivalent registrar, and holders restricted by
rule.

## The sequence

1. **[Settle the register of record](/docs/tokenization/design)** - whether the
   mint is the register or mirrors one held off-chain. Revisiting it after
   launch generally means a new mint and a migration.
2. **[Design the authorities](/docs/tokenization/design/program-and-authority-design)** -
   which key or multisig holds each authority, and how that maps onto the
   transfer agent's duties.
3. **[Choose transfer controls](/docs/tokenization/design/choosing-controls)** -
   the control surface an instrument with restricted holders needs.
4. **[Evidence holder eligibility](/docs/tokenization/compliance)** -
   attestations that evidence an eligibility decision without publishing the
   underlying data, and [Token ACL](/docs/tokenization/compliance/token-acl) to
   enforce it.
5. **[Integrate custody](/docs/tokenization/custody)** - the custodian
   relationship and the
   [issuer and transfer-agent boundary](/docs/tokenization/custody/transfer-agent-responsibilities).
6. **[Settle against payment](/docs/tokenization/settlement/dvp-program)** -
   exchanging the security against its cash leg in a single transaction through
   the DvP program's escrow, or through
   [delegated authority](/docs/tokenization/settlement/dvp) without a program.
   [Choose the cash leg](/docs/tokenization/settlement/cash-leg) covers which
   instrument settles against the security, and
   [settlement and finality](/docs/tokenization/settlement/finality) which
   commitment level to record as settled. Neither settlement page continues the
   tutorial mint: the program rejects the Scaled UI Amount extension that mint
   carries, and the extensions-only page builds its own mint.
7. **[Operate it](/docs/tokenization/tutorials)** - build a gated, operable mint
   and run the ongoing operations against it.
