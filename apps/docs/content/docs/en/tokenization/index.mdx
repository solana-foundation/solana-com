Solana is purpose-built for issuing and operating tokenized assets at scale:
sub-second confirmation, fees under $0.001, and a token standard with native
compliance controls - transfer restrictions, pausability, confidential
transfers, and permanent delegates - that on other chains require custom smart
contracts.

Mint accounts, token accounts, and the full extension surface are documented in
[Assets](/docs/tokens).

## The sequence

Whatever the asset, an issuance moves through the same decisions in roughly this
order.

1. **[Design the token](/docs/tokenization/design)** - whether the mint is your
   register of record or an off-chain register governs, which token program to
   issue on, how the asset shapes the control surface, and who holds each
   authority. Revisiting this after launch generally requires a new mint and a
   migration.
2. **[Choose where it runs](/docs/tokenization/design/deployment-options)** -
   whether the asset belongs on mainnet or on mainnet with Confidential
   Balances, and what the second option costs you in composability.
3. **[Prove compliance](/docs/tokenization/compliance)** - proving a KYC or
   sanctions check without putting personal data onchain, and what Confidential
   Balances hide, leave public, and cost you in control.
4. **[Gate the holders](/docs/tokenization/compliance/token-acl)** - enforcing
   allowlists and blocklists with Token ACL.
5. **[Integrate custody](/docs/tokenization/custody)** - how the asset meets a
   custodian and a transfer agent, and what a vault needs from the token before
   it can hold it.
6. **[Choose the cash leg](/docs/tokenization/settlement/cash-leg)** - which
   cash instrument settles against the asset, and how that choice and the
   controls on the mint constrain each other.
7. **[Settle it](/docs/tokenization/settlement)** - exchanging the asset against
   payment in a single transaction, and
   [which commitment level](/docs/tokenization/settlement/finality) a system of
   record should record.
8. **[Operate it](/docs/tokenization/tutorials)** - build a gated, operable
   mint, then take the custodian and vault sides of it.

Use the platform when you want one integrated surface across issuance, custody,
payments, and compliance. Use the direct tutorials when you need to understand,
inspect, or customize the underlying Token-2022 and Token ACL operations. The
two are complementary, not a maturity ladder.

and its [extensions](/docs/tokens/extensions). If you are new to Solana's
