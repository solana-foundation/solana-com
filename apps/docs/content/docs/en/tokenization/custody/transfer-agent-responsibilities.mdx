<Callout type="info" title="Summary">

The register-of-record decision from [token design](/docs/tokenization/design)
determines the division of labor. If the mint is the register, the token program
maintains the record of balances and the transfer agent's remaining authority is
operational; whether that discharges their record-keeping obligation is a
question for counsel. If an off-chain register governs, the mint is a mirror and
every onchain action needs a corresponding entry in the governing register.

</Callout>

## The two arrangements

### The mint is the register

The mint is the operative record of holdings, and no separate book competes with
it. Whether that record is the definitive register in law, and whether it
discharges the transfer agent's record-keeping obligation, are questions for
counsel, and regimes may differ; see
[settlement and finality](/docs/tokenization/settlement/finality).

The token program maintains the record of balances. What remains for the
transfer agent is everything that is not the record itself: identity and
eligibility, corporate actions, holder servicing, and statutory reporting. What
they hold onchain is a key that can add to the allowlist, or a co-signature on
mint and burn.

### An off-chain register governs

The existing book of record stays in place, and the mint mirrors it.

In this arrangement, each onchain action has a corresponding entry in the
governing register, and a reconciliation cadence between the two catches drift
before it reaches a holder. As one illustration, the transfer agent keeps their
existing function and also runs that reconciliation; who takes it on depends on
the arrangement.

## Dividing the duties

The split below is a starting point. Responsibilities may vary by institution.

| Duty                                     | Issuer                        | Transfer agent                             |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Deciding to issue or redeem              | Yes                           | Executes                                   |
| Holding the mint authority               | Yes                           | Sometimes a co-signer                      |
| Holder eligibility and KYC               | Sets the policy               | Runs the checks                            |
| Maintaining the allowlist                | Owns the gate                 | Operates it day to day where so arranged   |
| Signing mint and burn                    | Yes                           | Co-signs where the arrangement requires it |
| Cap-table maintenance                    |                               | Yes                                        |
| Reconciling onchain against the register | Shares                        | Shares                                     |
| Corporate actions                        | Declares                      | Executes                                   |
| Holder servicing and statements          |                               | Yes                                        |
| Exercising the permanent delegate        | Yes, under a governed process | Requests, does not hold                    |
| Statutory and regulatory reporting       | Yes                           | Supports                                   |

**Allowlist operation is the transfer agent's eligibility function, held as an
onchain list.** Where the eligibility decision is produced by a check they
already run, wiring it through an attestation keeps the evidence off-chain - see
[attestation-based compliance](/docs/tokenization/compliance/attestation-based-compliance).

## Reconciliation

What to compare:

- **Total supply** onchain against the register's total issued. This catches
  mint and burn errors.
- **Per-holder balances** against the register's positions, keyed by the address
  the register records for each holder.
- **The allowlist** against the register's eligible-holder list. Drift here may
  be an early sign that an onboarding or offboarding process has broken.
- **Frozen accounts** against the register's restricted positions.

Practical points:

- **Reconcile at a stated commitment level, not "now."** A read at default
  commitment can be superseded. Use finalized for anything that feeds a book of
  record - see
  [settlement and finality](/docs/tokenization/settlement/finality).
- **A permanent-delegate transfer moves a balance without holder instruction.**
- **Scaled UI amount changes the displayed balance without a transfer.** If your
  token uses it, reconcile raw amounts and apply the multiplier for
  presentation, or every rebase becomes a reconciliation exception. See
  [scaled UI amount](/docs/tokens/extensions/scaled-ui-amount).
- **Map holders to addresses off-chain.** One holder may control several
  addresses, and a custodian may hold for many. Nothing onchain provides this
  mapping.
