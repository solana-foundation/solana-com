<Info>
The register-of-record decision from
[token design](/developers/docs/tokenization/design) determines the division of
labor. If the mint is the register, the token program maintains the record of
balances and the transfer agent's remaining authority is operational; whether
that discharges their record-keeping obligation is a question for counsel. If
an offchain register governs, the mint is a mirror and every onchain action
needs a matching entry in the governing register.
</Info>

## The two arrangements

### The mint is the register

The mint is the operative record of holdings, and no separate book competes
with it. Whether that record is the definitive register in law, and whether it
discharges the transfer agent's record-keeping obligation, are questions for
counsel, and regimes may differ; see
[settlement and finality](/developers/docs/defi/finality#protocol-finality-is-not-legal-finality).

The token program maintains the record of balances. What remains for the
transfer agent is everything that is not the record itself: identity and
eligibility, corporate actions, holder servicing, and statutory reporting. What
they hold onchain is a key that can add to the allowlist, or a co-signature on
mint and burn.

### An offchain register governs

The existing book of record stays in place, and the mint mirrors it.

In this arrangement, each onchain action has a matching entry in the governing
register, and a reconciliation cadence between the two catches drift before it
reaches a holder. In one common arrangement, the transfer agent keeps their
existing function and also runs that reconciliation; who takes it on depends
on the agreement.

## Dividing the duties

The split below is a starting point. Responsibilities vary by institution.

| Duty                                     | Issuer                        | Transfer agent                             |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Deciding to issue or redeem              | Yes                           | Executes                                   |
| Holding the mint authority               | Yes                           | Sometimes a co-signer                      |
| Holder eligibility and KYC               | Sets the policy               | Runs the checks                            |
| Maintaining the allowlist                | Owns the gate                 | Operates it day to day where so arranged   |
| Signing mint and burn                    | Yes                           | Co-signs where the arrangement requires it |
| Cap-table maintenance                    |                               | Yes                                        |
| Reconciling onchain against the register | Shares                        | Shares                                     |
| Corporate actions                        | Declares                      | Executes                                   |
| Holder servicing and statements          |                               | Yes                                        |
| Exercising the permanent delegate        | Yes, under a governed process | Requests, does not hold                    |
| Statutory and regulatory reporting       | Yes                           | Supports                                   |

**Allowlist operation is the transfer agent's eligibility function, held as an
onchain list.** Where a check they already run produces the eligibility
decision, wiring it through an attestation keeps the evidence offchain; see
[attestation-based compliance](/developers/docs/tokenization/compliance/attestation-based-compliance).

## Reconciliation

What to compare:

- **Total supply** onchain against the register's total issued. This catches
  mint and burn errors.
- **Per-holder balances** against the register's positions, keyed by the
  address the register records for each holder.
- **The allowlist** against the register's list of eligible holders. Drift here
  may be an early sign that an onboarding or offboarding process has broken.
- **Frozen accounts** against the register's restricted positions.

Practical points:

- **Reconcile at a stated commitment level, not "now."** A read at a lower
  commitment can be superseded. Use `finalized` for anything that feeds a book
  of record; see [settlement and finality](/developers/docs/defi/finality).
- **A permanent-delegate transfer moves a balance without holder
  instruction.** Record it as an issuer action, not an unexplained movement.
- **Scaled UI Amount changes the displayed balance without a transfer.** If
  your token uses it, reconcile raw amounts and apply the multiplier for
  presentation, or every rebase becomes a reconciliation exception. See
  [Scaled UI Amount](/developers/docs/tokens/extensions/scaled-ui-amount).
- **Map holders to addresses offchain.** One holder may control several
  addresses, and a custodian may hold for many. Nothing onchain provides this
  mapping.
