<Callout type="info" title="Summary">

A gated Token-2022 mint makes demands an ordinary token does not, and every
third party that holds it - custodian, transfer agent, vault program - has to be
able to meet them. Confirming that comes before the token design is final.

</Callout>

## The three holders

**A custodian** holds the asset for someone else. Their systems have to create a
token account that starts frozen, get it thawed against your gate, and keep
working when the issuer exercises a control they do not hold. Support varies by
custodian and is specific to individual extensions rather than to Token-2022 as
a whole.

**A transfer agent** maintains the holder record. Where the mint is the register
of record, the record of balances is kept onchain and their onchain authority is
a signing key; where an off-chain register governs, they keep their existing
function and gain an obligation to keep the mirror from drifting.

**A vault** is a program that holds assets and issues claims against them. It is
subject to the gate like any other holder. Under an allowlist gate, the accounts
it touches have to be allowlisted before the first deposit.

## What they have in common

Each one needs the same thing from you: a way onto the allowlist with a named
contact and a committed turnaround, notice before an authority rotates, and
documentation of what each control does in terms their risk function can assess.
Each control the issuer can exercise unilaterally has to be detectable and
explainable by the holder.
