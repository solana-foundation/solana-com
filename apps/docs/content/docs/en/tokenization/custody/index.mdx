<Callout type="info" title="Summary">

Every third party that holds a gated Token-2022 mint (custodian, transfer agent,
vault program) has to support its controls.

</Callout>

## Custodian, transfer agent, and vault

**A custodian** holds the asset for someone else. A custodian's systems have to
handle a frozen-by-default account, the thaw against your gate, and controls the
issuer exercises;
[custody integration](/docs/tokenization/custody/custody-integration) lists what
a custodian has to support.

**A transfer agent** maintains the holder record. Where the mint is the register
of record, the record of balances is kept onchain and their onchain authority is
a signing key; where an off-chain register governs, they keep their existing
function and may also run the reconciliation that keeps the mirror from
drifting.

**A vault** is a program that holds assets and issues claims against them. It is
subject to the gate like any other holder. Under an allowlist gate, the
addresses that own the accounts it touches have to be allowlisted before the
first deposit.
