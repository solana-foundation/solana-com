<Info>
Every third party that holds a gated Token-2022 mint (a custodian, a transfer
agent, or a vault program) has to support its controls.
</Info>

## Custodian, transfer agent, and vault

**A custodian** holds the asset for someone else, and its systems have to
support the mint's controls;
[custody integration](/developers/docs/tokenization/custody/custody-integration)
lists what to confirm.

**A transfer agent** maintains the holder record. Where the mint is the
register of record, the record of balances is kept onchain and the transfer
agent's onchain authority is a signing key. Where an offchain register governs,
they keep their existing function and may also run the reconciliation that
keeps the mirror from drifting; see
[transfer agent responsibilities](/developers/docs/tokenization/custody/transfer-agent-responsibilities).

**A vault** is a program that holds assets and issues claims against them. It
is subject to the gate like any other holder. Under an allowlist gate, the
addresses that own the accounts it touches have to be allowlisted before the
first deposit; see
[vault architectures](/developers/docs/tokenization/custody/vault-architectures).
