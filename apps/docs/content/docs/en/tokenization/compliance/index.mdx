A regulated token raises two separate problems, and each has its own page.

**Evidencing a decision.** A gated token needs an onchain answer to "may this
address hold the token?" without putting the personal data behind that answer
on a public ledger.
[Attestation-based compliance](/developers/docs/tokenization/compliance/attestation-based-compliance)
covers how.

**Concealing a value.** Confidential Balances encrypt amounts and balances
while leaving addresses, the mint, and the timing of every transfer public.
Once amounts are encrypted, a rule that depends on the size of a transfer
cannot be enforced onchain.
[Privacy and transfer controls](/developers/docs/tokenization/compliance/privacy-and-transfer-controls)
covers which issuer controls still work.

Both can run on the same mint. If counterparty relationships are sensitive,
compare the solutions in [Private payments](/developers/docs/finance/privacy)
and the
[deployment options](/developers/docs/tokenization/design/deployment-options).
