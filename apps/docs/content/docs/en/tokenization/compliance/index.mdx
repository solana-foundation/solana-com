<Callout type="info" title="Summary">

Attestations evidence that a check happened without publishing what it was based
on. The confidential transfer extension keeps amounts confidential. Neither
hides who transacted with whom, and encrypted amounts make any rule you write
that depends on the size of a transfer unenforceable onchain.

</Callout>

## Two separate problems

**Evidencing a decision.** A gated token needs an onchain answer to "may this
address hold the token?" without putting the personal data behind that answer on
a public ledger;
[attestation-based compliance](/docs/tokenization/compliance/attestation-based-compliance)
covers how.

**Concealing a value.** Confidential Balances encrypt amounts and balances while
leaving addresses, the mint, and the timing of every transfer public;
[privacy and transfer controls](/docs/tokenization/compliance/privacy-and-transfer-controls)
covers which issuer controls still work.

Both can run on the same mint. If counterparty relationships are sensitive,
compare the models in [Privacy on Solana](/docs/finance/privacy) and the
[deployment options](/docs/tokenization/design/deployment-options).
