Privacy on Solana is a set of application-level tools rather than a single
network mode. Choose the primitive that matches what your product needs to hide,
who must be able to inspect it, and where settlement occurs.

<Callout type="info" title="Privacy has explicit boundaries">
  Confidential transfers hide token amounts and balances, not account addresses
  or transaction participation. Define your disclosure, audit, and key-custody
  requirements before choosing an integration path.
</Callout>
