## What You'll Build

- **Foundations** — a local environment and your first onchain program.
- **Program patterns** — onchain voting, a token escrow, and private transfers.
- **Token projects** — a stablecoin, a stable swap AMM, and real-world assets.
- **Payments and apps** — an x402 payment gateway and a full-stack prediction
  market.
- **Shipping** — a security checklist, indexing, and production readiness.
