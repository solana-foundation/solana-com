## Chapters

Each chapter is also published as its own episode, if you'd rather watch one
project at a time.

### Introduction

How the bootcamp is organized, and the blockchain mental model behind Solana.
Starts at 0:00:00 in the full course. Episodes: [Bootcamp
Intro](https://youtu.be/0pp7HyTadPg) (2:08) and [Intro to
Blockchain](https://youtu.be/rW4zWO08ttc) (6:10).

### Local Installation

Install the bootcamp toolchain and confirm your local setup works. Starts at
0:08:18 in the full course. [Watch the episode](https://youtu.be/_Um3PGeTNI4)
(8:07).

### Hello World

Start from `create-solana-dapp` and ship your first full-stack Solana app.
Starts at 0:16:25 in the full course. [Watch the
episode](https://youtu.be/LkA2PPbKK3o) (6:59).

### AI Best Practices

Build with AI tools while you check the blockchain-critical details yourself.
Starts at 0:23:26 in the full course. [Watch the
episode](https://youtu.be/pk8iIZiGKtM) (7:40).

### Onchain Voting

Build a voting program to practice initializing and updating account state.
Starts at 0:31:05 in the full course. [Watch the
episode](https://youtu.be/Pwq6Kc7MlC0) (49:42).

### Escrow Application

Implement an escrow with explicit state transitions, PDAs, and authority checks.
Starts at 1:20:45 in the full course. [Watch the
episode](https://youtu.be/lWyrONFrBuI) (29:34).

### Private Transfers

Build a full-stack private transfer flow and weigh its UX, state, and privacy
tradeoffs. Starts at 1:50:23 in the full course. [Watch the
episode](https://youtu.be/ElCcz76ZnSE) (1:01:08).

### Stablecoin

Create a stablecoin-style token and manage its mint, authorities, and supply.
Starts at 2:51:33 in the full course. [Watch the
episode](https://youtu.be/GKxYdyD1Otk) (15:59).

### Stable Swap

Implement a stable swap and the rules that keep pool balances and pricing
consistent. Starts at 3:07:32 in the full course. [Watch the
episode](https://youtu.be/7KloeSesgPw) (26:14).

### x402

Charge for app requests with x402 onchain payments. Starts at 3:33:47 in the
full course. [Watch the episode](https://youtu.be/-TGLnDP0U5k) (13:47).

### Real-World Assets

Apply the bootcamp stack to a real-world asset use case and its product
constraints. Starts at 3:47:34 in the full course. [Watch the
episode](https://youtu.be/WLQkD7e-te8) (22:48).

### Security Checklist

Check the patterns from the earlier projects against a list of checks to make
before shipping. Starts at 4:10:23 in the full course. [Watch the
episode](https://youtu.be/UgUPbdGWowM) (2:37).

### Indexing

Index onchain data so your app can query and display it reliably. Starts at
4:13:01 in the full course. [Watch the episode](https://youtu.be/cQY320Mo-HQ)
(3:07).

### Prediction Market

Ship a prediction market with market mechanics, user actions, and app-level
guardrails. Starts at 4:16:07 in the full course. [Watch the
episode](https://youtu.be/c3qgfZmdbc0) (45:42).

### Production Readiness

Review the deployment, monitoring, security, and team processes that take a
prototype to production. Starts at 5:01:52 in the full course. [Watch the
episode](https://youtu.be/ezaNcGLcruU) (9:44).
