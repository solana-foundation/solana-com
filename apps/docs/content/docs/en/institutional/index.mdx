This section is for a regulated institution considering issuing a token on
Solana. It assumes you have, or are arranging, a legal wrapper and a compliance
function, along with a custodian where the token records an asset held
elsewhere, and that your questions are about how the asset behaves onchain and
what your reviewers may ask about it.

These pages cover the same primitives documented elsewhere for building a
product, sequenced instead by the decisions an issuance typically involves and
annotated with the constraints that can apply when the token is a liability on a
balance sheet.

## The sequence

1. **[Token design](/docs/institutional/token-design)** - whether the mint is
   your register of record or an off-chain register governs, which token program
   to issue on, how the asset shapes the control surface, and who holds each
   authority. Revisiting this after launch generally requires a new mint and a
   migration.
2. **[Compliance & privacy](/docs/institutional/compliance-and-privacy)** -
   proving a KYC or sanctions check without putting personal data onchain, and
   what confidential balances hide, leave public, and cost you in control.
3. **[Architecture patterns](/docs/institutional/architecture)** - whether the
   asset belongs on mainnet, on mainnet with confidential balances, or somewhere
   further from the public network, and what each step costs.
4. **[Custody & vaults](/docs/institutional/custody-and-vaults)** - how the
   asset meets a custodian and a transfer agent, and what a vault needs from the
   token before it can hold it.
5. **[Payments and interoperability](/docs/institutional/payments-and-interoperability)** -
   which cash instrument settles against the asset, and how that choice and the
   controls on the mint constrain each other.
6. **[Tutorials](/docs/institutional/tutorials)** - build a gated, operable
   mint, then take the custodian and vault sides of it.
7. **[Settlement and finality](/docs/institutional/settlement-finality)** -
   which commitment level to use for a system of record, and which one suits a
   user-facing interface.
8. **[Risk and diligence](/docs/institutional/risk-and-diligence)** - common
   questions from risk, legal, and compliance reviewers, with what is known and
   what remains open.
