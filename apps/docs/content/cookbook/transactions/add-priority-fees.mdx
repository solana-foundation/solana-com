A priority fee is an optional fee paid to increase the chance that the current
leader processes your transaction.

To add a priority fee to a transaction, invoke instructions from the
[Compute Budget Program](https://github.com/solana-program/compute-budget).

> The
> [v1 transaction format](/developers/docs/core/transactions/versioned-transactions#v1-format)
> handles this differently: ComputeBudget instructions are ignored (they execute
> as no-ops), and the priority fee is set directly in the message config as a
> **total in lamports** rather than a price in micro-lamports per compute unit.
>
> Because those are different quantities, `@solana/kit` gives them separate
> setters and enforces the split in the type system:
> `setTransactionMessagePriorityFeeLamports` accepts only v1 messages, and
> `setTransactionMessageComputeUnitPrice` accepts only `legacy` and `0`. The
> other resource limits are version-agnostic —
> `setTransactionMessageComputeUnitLimit`,
> `setTransactionMessageLoadedAccountsDataSizeLimit`, and
> `setTransactionMessageHeapSize` take a message of any version.
