To send transactions while developing locally or on devnet, you'll need SOL.

On a local validator, such as Surfpool or `solana-test-validator`, you can fund
an address by requesting an airdrop, as the examples below do. The public devnet
and testnet endpoints no longer serve airdrops, so on devnet use the
[faucet](https://faucet.solana.com/) instead (see
[more ways to get devnet SOL](/developers/cookbook/development/airdrops-and-faucets)).
