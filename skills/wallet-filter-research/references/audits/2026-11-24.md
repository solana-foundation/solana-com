# Wallet directory audit — 24 November 2026

[ECO-1022](https://linear.app/solana-fndn/issue/ECO-1022/refresh-agent-context-and-audit-the-solana-wallet-directory)
tracks this audit and the companion agent-context refresh.

The [evidence matrix](./2026-11-24.json) covers all 158 baseline records and one
discovery addition. It records the ten reviewed fields, before/after
corrections, product sources, uncertainty, and a separate security assessment
for each wallet. The resulting directory still has 158 records: 32 updated, 125
carried forward, Samui removed, and Samsung Wallet Stablecoin Account added.

## Reading the evidence

Every baseline record received a canonical-site review, identity/field review,
and current adverse-evidence search. Deeper checks targeted changed, ambiguous,
and high-impact claims through official documentation, distribution listings,
repositories, disclosures, regulators and security researchers. Name collisions
were checked separately, including Nova, Omni, Ethos, Portal, Porto, Lemon,
PumpWallet, OWallet and Umbra.

`reviewedFields` contains the baseline values; `changes` supplies the proposed
corrections. For additions, `reviewedFields` contains the proposed record.
Fields without changes are carried forward with the qualifications in
`evidence`, not claimed to be independently re-proven. Missing filter tags do
not establish that a product lacks a capability. This is a full-directory
editorial review, not a penetration test or an exhaustive certification of every
absent filter.

Some official pages blocked automated access, redirected, or left detailed
capabilities unclear. Those limits remain explicit in the matrix. All surviving
existing `lastVerified` dates are retained; completing the review queue does not
justify stamping every record as fully verified today. Only the new, narrowly
scoped Samsung record receives the current date.

Search results and review complaints supplied leads. They are not findings by
themselves. The matrix distinguishes opened primary reports from unverified or
blocked-source leads. No wallets were installed, connected to funds, or
exercised with transactions. “No material signal found” never means safe.

## Material publishing decisions

- Remove Samui: its
  [maintainer repository](https://github.com/samui-build/samui-wallet) is
  archived and explicitly describes an unmaintained experiment unsuitable for
  real funds. Remove its unused icon as well.
- Add
  [Samsung Wallet Stablecoin Account](https://www.samsung.com/us/apps/samsung-wallet/stablecoin/):
  custodial USDC on Solana and Sui, Android, direct fiat purchases and
  withdrawals. The [FAQ](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10013605/)
  distinguishes Samsung's app, Bastion's service and Coinbase institutional
  custody. Eligibility is limited to supported U.S. Galaxy users; New York is
  excluded. Existing Samsung Pay cards do not prove spending from this USDC
  account. Its short operating history is documented separately.
- Rename the stable `emcd` record to Coinhold Wallet. Official
  [rebrand notice](https://emcd.io/articles/news-en/emcd-mining-and-coinhold-wallet-are-now-separate-products/)
  and [terms](https://coinhold.io/terms-of-service/) establish the current
  product and custodial keys. Remove unsubstantiated NFT support and replace the
  icon using the official Coinhold favicon. Preserve the old name as an alias.
- Remove Salmon's retired web platform, retain its Chrome and Android beta
  surfaces, and add documented collectibles support. Keep TipLink's maintenance
  mode and Phantom SDK's paused new-app onboarding visible in their
  descriptions.
- Correct Trustee Wallet's link to its own product documentation and remove the
  separate Trustee Plus card attribution. Remove Coin98's
  signing-card/payment-card confusion, CoinFlip's ATM cash-out tag, and
  Republic's external-exchange off-ramp tag. Narrow CFX to its current
  settlement product.
- Add documented mobile/browser/API platforms and direct payment capabilities.
  Nabox multisig is explicitly qualified as EVM Safe support; TokenPocket's card
  funding is documented on Arbitrum. Product filters do not imply every
  capability exists on every supported chain.

## Security dispositions and follow-up

Across 159 reviewed subjects, the current assessment is: 14 heightened concern,
70 monitor, 21 insufficient history, and 54 no material signal found. Historical
compromised releases are identified as such without labeling a remediated
current product malicious. No currently published product was established to be
deliberately harmful by this review.

The heightened-concern subjects are **Atomic, Coin98, CoinFlip, Cropty,
Cryptomus, Cwallet, DCENT, Ledger, Liminal, NGRAVE, Samui, TipLink, Trust Wallet
and Web3Auth**. Samui is removed for affirmative maintenance/availability
reasons. The remaining entries are retained pending operator or incident
follow-up; the registry has no reviewed risk-disclosure field. Their security
concerns are not inserted as unqualified accusations into marketing copy.

Prioritize these unresolved questions using the linked matrix sources:

- Cryptomus: FINTRAC enforcement and the UK sanctions notice are material
  operator concerns. A maintainer should separately review continued directory
  inclusion.
- Atomic, Coin98, DCENT, Ledger, Liminal, NGRAVE, Trust Wallet and Web3Auth:
  establish current affected versions/accounts, recovery status and mitigations.
  Preserve hardware versus software, current versus legacy, reseller versus
  official supply, and ECDSA versus EdDSA distinctions.
- Cropty: the FCA warning is an authorization finding for specified
  jurisdictions, not a fraud judgment. CoinFlip and Cwallet regulator leads were
  readable through indexed text but blocked on direct open; obtain the
  underlying orders or alerts before extending the conclusions.
- TipLink: confirm the long-term maintenance/migration plan. Moongate's parked
  domain and Unruggable's waitlist also need availability follow-up; neither is
  removed solely because a usable current product was hard to establish.
- The matrix flags remaining field gaps, including model-specific mobile
  support, legacy account availability, source-code availability and ambiguous
  fiat/card flows. No unsupported feature is newly added to resolve those gaps.
